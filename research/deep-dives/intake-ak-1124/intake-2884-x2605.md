## 2. Key claims

0. **Backend alone shifts benchmark scores by up to 16.6 points (CONFIRMED as arithmetic, OVERTURNED as a numerics claim).** The 16.60 spread is DeepSeek R1 Distill Qwen 7B on GSM8K, best vs worst backend. The authors trace the largest gaps to engine defaults, not arithmetic: Ollama prepends a BOS token and applies repetition penalty 1.1; setting the penalty to 1.0 alone raises that cell by 11.67 points, and BOS removal gives +7.35. Excluding Ollama and LMDeploy, the same cell spans 78.24-78.47 (0.23 points). Anchors: page, abstract, "the choice of backend alone can shift benchmark scores by up to 16.6"; section 6.1, "Ollama prepends a beginning-of-sequence (BOS) token"; "Setting it to 1.0 1.0 raises GSM8K accuracy by 11.67 11.67 points for DeepSeek R1 Distill Qwen 7B".
1. **llama.cpp vs transformers magnitudes (CONFIRMED from Table 1, dive arithmetic).** Setup: FP16 (GGUF-FP16 for llama.cpp), one H100, greedy, batch size one, five models 4B-30B, four benchmarks, 20 cells. llama.cpp minus transformers: GSM8K -0.38 to 0.00 (all five models); GPQA -3.03 to +3.03; SimpleQA -1.00 to +0.35; LiveCodeBench -4.00 (Qwen3 Thinking 30B) to +2.29. Median absolute difference 0.48 points; maximum 4.00. llama.cpp shows 0.00 std on every cell, i.e. a deterministic single result per cell. GPQA has 198 items so one item is 0.51 points: the +/-3.03 cells are 6 items, +/-1.01 cells are 2 items. Anchors: page, section 5.1 Table 1 row "Qwen3 4B GPQA 35.86 ... 38.89"; section 4.2, "Evaluations use a batch size of one to prevent batching-induced instability".
2. **Optimization-induced drift is real but has no consistent direction, and no single failing layer (CONFIRMED).** Prefix caching toggled: DeepSeek GPQA -0.51 with llama.cpp (one item) and +3.54 with Ollama; "llama.cpp and Ollama also accumulate intermediate matrix multiplications in FP32"; layer tracking found "no single failing layer" with drift accumulating across layers. Anchors: page, section 6.2, "We found no single failing layer. Instead, slight numerical drifts caused by custom kernels accumulate across layers"; "llama.cpp and Ollama also accumulate intermediate matrix multiplications in FP32"; footnote, "GPQA contains 198 198 examples, so one net change in correctness corresponds to approximately 0.51 0.51 percentage points".
3. **Variance persists under sampling (CONFIRMED, narrowed).** At T=0.7 on GSM8K only (two models), Max-Min is 8.70 and 16.30, "nearly identical" to greedy, but those spreads are again Ollama and LMDeploy dominated. ## 8. Contradiction / adversarial pass

- Headline overturned for numerics: the paper itself attributes the largest gaps to correctable defaults (Ollama BOS and repetition penalty 1.1), so "backend as silent hyperparameter" is largely configuration, not kernels.
- Authors' limitations in the text: one GPU class for the main table (H100; L40 ablation), engines pinned to one version each, models 4B-30B, no batching or speculative decoding for llama.cpp, accuracy differences on GPQA (198 items) are single-digit item counts.
- llama.cpp cells have std 0.00, so no error bar exists for the +/-3.03 GPQA differences (6 items); do not read them as significant.
- Independent corroboration: exactpaper (intake-2872) and divloc (2608.13756) cite or agree in direction; neither adds llama.cpp data.
