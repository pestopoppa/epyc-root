### Dive corrections

- 2026-09-15 Stage-2b. Verification changed six things relative to how the brief and the surfacing entry framed this source. (1) The Split-WY hoist is mostly not Kachua's. The paper's Listing 8 computes T, W and U for every chunk before the sequential chunk loop. ### Verdict

Treat arXiv 2406.06484 (NeurIPS 2024) plus FLA @ 8ee22533 as the reference design that our rescued chunked GDN (719a8529d) implements. TAKE: (a) Hoisting state-independent intra-chunk terms (inverse/W/U; gates in log-difference form) once per (seq, head, chunk), parallel across chunks, is reference practice. Our column-grouped port recomputes them 8x per v-head, which is a documented platform tradeoff and not the paper's algorithm, so a hoisted prepass restores the reference. (b) Kachua's truly novel parts are only the QK^T hoist, GVA gram sharing, the Neumann inverse and 1/G. Of these, only the first two are portable. (c) Before trusting any chunked-GDN numerics or A/B at H=32, prove dispatch: at defaults on MI210, H=32 n_seqs=1 does not launch the chunked kernel, the G16 H=32 cases in 719a8529d test the recurrent kernel, and MIN_BLOCKS_PER_SM is compile-time only. DO NOT: cite the paper or FLA as evidence that chunked GDN can meet our 1e-7 NMSE gate (FLA tolerates about 2.5e-5); port Triton/TF32/bf16 storage choices; port the paper's ratio notation literally (use log differences); or attribute the 2e-7 T=2048 miss to #24561 (it was #26001 plus a patch).
adopt_patterns: adopt the reference factorisation in which state-independent intra-chunk terms are computed once per (seq, v-head, chunk) and consumed by the state walk, in log-difference gating form. Do not adopt FLA's Triton autotune/TF32/bf16 storage choices, per-chunk state storage, or tolerance.
