### Verdict

The operative finding is that the graded tie-free signal is obtainable from TEXT ratings plus margin weighting, with no logprob plumbing - roughly twenty lines, training-free, deployable on any llama-server. At our 3-to-5 pool sizes use exhaustive round-robin plus an order swap plus margin-weighted aggregation; do NOT build V1 Swiss scheduler, which is provably inert at N of 7 or below, and do NOT build the pivot tournament either. Never repeat the claim that V1 own budget curve is non-monotonic - V1 published only to 3N and claims the opposite; the 5N and 7N points are a third-party extrapolation beyond its envelope. The repo is unlicensed, so reimplement from the paper equations.
