### Claims (as verified)

0. A reference-free judge shown a candidate answer without ground truth scores plausibility, not correctness — 'verification asymmetry' — creating false-positive basins of convincing-but-wrong answers.

1. Optimizing a policy against such judge verdicts (self-play + DPO) drives judge-measured pass rates up while independently-verified accuracy stays flat; all measured gain is reward hacking.
   - *narrowed*: CONFIRMED for the Qwen3-4B headline, but "all measured gain is reward hacking" states an inevitability the paper explicitly disclaims: under DPO, entry into the basin is stochastic (3 of 5 seeds hack; 2 are clean matched negative controls). Its signature is reliable, its occurrence is not.

4. Requiring the judge to commit to its own independent answer before (or without) seeing the candidate — 'de-anchoring' — collapses false positives and prevents the basin forming during training.

(1) The de-anchoring fix has a CAPABILITY THRESHOLD: it HURTS a judge that cannot solve the task, helping only larger judges and plateauing by 8B. (2) On CODE the fix leaves a large residual - de-anchored gap@16 floors at 0.225-0.227 even at 14B, never approaching zero, whereas the headline FPR collapse 0.906 -> 0.012 is a MATH/exact-match result. Proposition 2 proves any non-decreasing aggregation over reference-free verdicts is monotone in the shared plausibility signal and bounded below by the product of individual FPRs. Min-vote is the strictest monotone rule and still fails; majority-k, weighted vote and Bradley-Terry consensus are all inside the proposition. On code the three-judge Min ensemble accepts 16.4 percent of wrong programs, 2.38x the independence prediction. Training against the ensemble makes it worse (Min FPR 41.2 -> 73.3 percent). Consequence for our own doctrine: cross-family verification remains valid against self-favouring/collusion bias but is FALSIFIED as an anti-reward- hacking defence. The axis that works is independence-from-the-candidate, not diversity-of-judge. RECONCILIATION VERDICT: (2) BOUNDS - not refutes, not orthogonal. In the very experiment where the judge-measured gap reaches 0.588, the TRUE unit-test pass rate of the selected candidate still rises 0.27 -> 0.29 - a small real gain beside a large fictitious one. ### Verdict

This BOUNDS judge-guided best-of-N; it does not refute it, and it is not orthogonal. The training-free-proxy result means selection-only is NOT a safe harbour. Carry the five conditions B1-B5 recorded in dive_corrections with any citation, and in particular carry B5: the selection arm of LLM-as-a-Verifier is bounded, but its RL-dense-reward arm sits in this paper''s refuted regime. Do NOT reach for a cross-family local ensemble as a mitigation - Proposition 2 excludes every monotone aggregation rule, and this is a 1967 association inequality, not a preprint heuristic. Do NOT deploy commit-first on our reviewer pool before measuring reviewer solve-accuracy: below the capability threshold it degrades the judge.
adopt_patterns — the value is protocol, not code. It offers a practical mitigation adoptable as an eval-tower rule, in two parts. (1) ANCHOR RULE: any judge-scored metric used to gate a decision must be paired with a judge-independent verifier (exact-match, unit tests, code execution, or a held-out anchor), and the judge-vs-anchor gap reported alongside the judge number; a widening gap under increasing N is the reward-hacking signature. (2) DE-ANCHORING RULE: where a judge must be used for selection, run it commit-first or blind-solve rather than the current show-the-candidate-and-ask pattern — a bolt-on prompt change with a large reported effect (FPR 0.906 -> 0.012), noting that a plain double-check instruction is measured to be worthless. Concretely this should (a) become a PROPOSED rule in eval-tower-verification.md next to the existing cross-family rule, with the ensemble caveat recorded against it; (b) gate any re-enablement of graded Claude-as-Judge on an anchored gap report; (c) inform EV-9 judge-role selection and RM-5 robustness probes; (d) trigger a retrospective check of whether any autopilot best-of-N gain rests on judge-measured scores alone. NOT adopt_component: no released artifact, single-author preprint, numbers not protocol-certified.
