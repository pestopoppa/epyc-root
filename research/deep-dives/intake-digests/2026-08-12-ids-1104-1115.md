Stage 1 filed this entry as predicting that our hand-built topology sits on the WINNING side of the paper split. The mechanism the paper actually isolates is LLM-MEDIATED COORDINATION VERSUS DETERMINISTIC CODE COORDINATION, plus NARROW FUNCTIONAL ROLES VERSUS PERSONA ROLES. "Expert-architected" is not a class of systems: it is ONE bespoke pipeline hand-built by the paper own authors for the one synthetic dataset those same authors designed, whose sub-agents are narrow single-function units (a Meta-Agent that is "a structural parser... but performs no numerical reasoning itself", an ExtractorAgent acting as "a high-precision filter", a CalculatorAgent), and whose load-bearing architectural move is REMOVING THE LLM FROM ORCHESTRATION ENTIRELY (see claim_anchors) with final win-determination "performed via deterministic Python logic". AGAINST THAT AXIS OUR TOPOLOGY SITS ON THE CRITICIZED SIDE OF FOUR OF FIVE DIMENSIONS: who manages the handoff (they: Python control script; us: LLM coordinator and LLM main), who makes the final decision (they: deterministic logic; us: LLM main integrating subagent results), role design (they: narrow functional units; us: curated domain-persona role files), and task-specificity of the decomposition (they: built for one known task shape; us: general-purpose roles reused across heterogeneous tasks). WE MATCH THE WINNER ON EXACTLY ONE DIMENSION — parallel fan-out over DISJOINT work items with genuine context separation, which is real and is the only structural match. THE ROLE-ABLATION EVIDENCE, CORRECTLY SCOPED (operator corrections, 2026-08-12 — the main initial framing of this was WRONG ON TWO COUNTS and is retracted here).
