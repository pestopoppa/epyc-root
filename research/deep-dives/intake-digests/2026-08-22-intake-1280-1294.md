### Verdict

Cite the 32-TOKEN TRUNCATION FINDING and never the paper's MaxSim architectural-ceiling framing — the dive overturned it. The plateau at 20 words is a query_maxlen = 32 CONFIGURATION artefact, established from the paper's own Introduction, from run_ablation_colbert.py:58, from the official ColBERT default, from metrics bit-identical to 17 significant figures across five truncation lengths in the authors' own results file, and from a direct measurement over all 622 official ToT test queries showing the encoded prefix is identical for 622/622 between 40 words and every longer condition.
