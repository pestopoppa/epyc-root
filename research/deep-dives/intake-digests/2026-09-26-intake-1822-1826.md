### Verdict

Code-verified compiler behaviour, no performance data. On ROCm 6.2 our <=256-thread MFMA kernels pick AGPR form (and pay v_accvgpr copies) whenever their register ceiling exceeds 256; a register cap (launch-bounds min-blocks 2 or amdgpu_num_vgpr) flips them to VGPR form today, as the occupancy-2 MMA-FA kernels in our own binary already show. ROCm 7.14/10.x flip the default for all. MMQ (512 threads) is unaffected either way. Any change must pass a static register audit before timing.
worth_investigating: the net performance effect of removing copies vs the 256-register cap and spills is unmeasured on our kernels.
