## intake-1215#record — TALE: Token-Budget-Aware LLM Reasoning

- **URL**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.18547
- **Verification**: dive-verified
- **Verdict**: new_opportunity (novelty medium, relevance high; categories: llm_prompting, context_management, cost_aware_routing, routing_intelligence)
- **Expanded from**: intake-1212#record

### Claims (as verified)

0. Current LLM reasoning is unnecessarily lengthy and can be compressed by placing an explicit token budget in the prompt, but the CHOICE of budget determines how much compression is actually achieved.
1. The budget is estimated PER QUESTION rather than fixed: by default the reasoning model itself is used as its own budget estimator via a zero-shot estimation prompt, so the intervention adapts to per-problem complexity.
2. Two variants are offered - TALE-EP (estimate-and-prompt, inference-time only, zero training) and TALE-PT (post-training via SFT or DPO).
3. The inference-time variant reaches roughly two-thirds token reduction at under three points of accuracy loss, and on some datasets IMPROVES accuracy while cutting tokens.
   - anchor `results / abstract-level summary of TALE-EP, as rendered by ar5iv` @ arXiv:2412.18547v5 (2025-06-02), read via ar5iv 2026-08-21: "TALE-EP achieves 67% reduction in token usage while maintaining accuracy with less than a 3% decrease."

All headline figures confirmed against the full text. NOTHING WAS OVERTURNED - this is a correctly-filed source and the dive says so plainly rather than manufacturing a finding. TWO THINGS THE DIVE ADDED THAT THE ABSTRACT DOES NOT GIVE. (1) THE GENERALISATION ROWS ARE WORSE THAN THE HEADLINE: MathBench-College shows -8% for GPT-4o-mini and -4% for GPT-4o against vanilla CoT, versus the -2.72% average. A chat template is executable code sitting in the prompt-construction position.
