## Bounding the regime for Zen 5 (EPYC 9655)
- Device: 25.6 GB/s spec LPDDR4, 4 A57 cores. Bandwidth per core about 6 GB/s, and the whole device is instruction-bound for 8-bit decode. Zen 5 here: read-sum 410-447 GB/s at t48-t96 (cpu-decode-roofline-program.md:418 @ ac1347f), about 4.3-9 GB/s per core of streaming but with far more ALU per byte than an A57; the dense path already runs 186-207 GB/s (:316).
- Implied decode rate: 3.8 G weights in 1.66 s on 4 threads = about 0.57 G weights/s/thread = ~0.4 symbols/cycle at 1.43 GHz for 16-symbol codes; for uint8, 6.66 s = ~0.1 symbol/cycle. Even tripling that for Zen 5 (about 1-1.5 sym/cycle at ~4 GHz, my assumption, not measured), in-loop decode on the Q38FN dense path (81 M symbols per core per eval, LazyWeights dive) is ~10-15 ms of added work for ~1 ms saved. The decline stands.
- A one-time unpack (the EntroLLM design) would only save disk or page-cache bytes; capacity is not a constraint (12 x 96 GB, cpu-decode-roofline-program.md:387).
- MI210: no HBM-path decoder, 1.6 TB/s; same conclusion as the LazyWeights dive.
