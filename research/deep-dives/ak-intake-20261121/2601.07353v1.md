**Mechanism.** The speedup model is R = tau/(1 + c·delta), where delta is drafter forwards per verify step. App E.6 states the gain "derives from its superior Draft Efficiency". The model assumes target verify time does not depend on tree size (eq. 9). Large-batch, compute-bound regimes are flagged as untested (Sec 8).

## Grounding

- **TALON's N=60 does not carry over to the MI210.** At s ≈ 1.8-2.4 ms/row, about 52 extra rows add roughly 95-125 ms to a 49-52 ms round, while TALON's gain in accepted tokens is at most about 10%.
- Converted with `program.md:L704-708`, this is a large loss.
- The program.md formula is exact only if a is the **fractional** increase in tokens per round. Read literally ("extra accepted tokens per step"), it overstates the gain by about 4.65x on the 27B. The stated breakeven of s ≈ 1.5 was computed with a relative a, so only the wording needs fixing.
- arXiv 2510.26577 (inference-cost-aware dynamic trees) is already in the intake index. It is the right framing for adding the verify cost TALON omits.
