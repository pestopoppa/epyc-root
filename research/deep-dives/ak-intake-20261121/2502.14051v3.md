## What RocketKV is
Stage 1: SnapKV permanent eviction of the **prompt** KV (observation window 32, per-GQA-group, pooling kernel **63** rather than SnapKV's 7). Stage 2: hybrid sparse attention (HSA) top-k on the survivors. HSA keeps per page the element-wise K max and K min (Quest), and for a query reads only the k1 head dims with largest |q| summed over the GQA group, choosing max or min by sign of q (SparQ). Then exact attention on the top-k2 tokens. Compression c is split c^r / c^(1-r), r = min(0.2 + 0.06 log2 c, 0.8); HSA splits its share evenly between page size and head-dim fraction. RocketKV-MT keeps all KV (no eviction) for multi-turn and only restricts attention.

## The settle-questions
**Is HSA's scorer our "persistent low-dimension key cache"? No.** The LSA finding (long-context-program.md, LSA-CPU-SPARSE note, "missing pieces are a persistent BF16 64-d index-key cache") is a learned/PCA projection of K to 64 dims, scored per key. HSA stores no projected key: it stores two page-level bound tensors (max, min) over raw dims and reads a data-dependent subset of dims per query. Both avoid re-reading full K at scoring time, but HSA's scoring is per page (page = c^((1-r)/2), e.g. 3 at 64x), so it does not scan every key. Storage: 2/c^((1+r)/2) of KV (Table 1). Because it needs no distillation it would not depend on LSA-QUALITY-32K (parked). Its quality at tight budget alone is weak (HSA-only NIAH 44.3 at 256), it needs stage 1.
**What quality at which budgets:** see claim 1; no number exists for hybrid models, reasoning traces, or Qwen3.x; budgets are in tokens per attention group (256..4096, 1024..16384 for SCBench).
**What the paper says about permanent eviction:** it is the weak link (multi-turn). For single-turn it works only because stage 1 is a **prompt-end query-aware pooled** selection; that is different from our EA, which scores keys against a mean key with no neighbourhood term.

## Adversarial pass
WebSearch 1 query: no independent replication, no reasoning-model evaluation, no third-party critique found. The paper's own caveats: eviction hurts multi-turn; HSA alone is weak at 256; accuracy gap to Exact-TopK persists in SCBench. RoCoKV's Obs 1 (intake-2760 dive) shows RocketKV's selection harms grow with uncertainty on R1 distills, i.e. the long-context result does not carry to reasoning decode.
