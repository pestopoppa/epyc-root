## Verified facts
- Appendix B.1 Table 2 (the only 'Table 2'; Stage-1 called it a main-text peak table): MATH-L5 GRPO 91.0, ROAM 89.8, intersection 88.0, union 92.8; Omni-MATH-L5/6 GRPO 41.3, ROAM (delayed) 46.0, intersection 36.5, union 50.8. On Omni the 4.8 pp lower intersection shows the two models solve different problems; with n=400 and a single training run, the per-problem union (50.8) is larger than either.
- Main training dynamics: GRPO peaks 91.0 (step 5000, MATH) and 41.3 (step 5000, Omni); ROAM peaks 89.8 (step 4500) and 43.2 (step 5500); delayed ROAM 46.0 at step 6000 with fluctuation afterwards. Error bars (95% bootstrap CIs) are over problems, not seeds.
- Training setup: Qwen3.5-2B (thinking off), LoRA r=8 alpha=16, GRPO G=8, beta=0, k=G=8, H=16, lambda=0.2, judge Qwen3.5-9B, lr 1e-5 constant, 2 prompts per step, max 3072 new tokens, temperature 1.0; evaluation on 400 MATH-L5 and 400 Omni-MATH L5/6 test problems; "All training runs were performed using an A100x8 cluster". No wall-clock, GPU-hour or judge-token cost is given. Delayed ROAM starts from the step-5000 GRPO checkpoint.
- Judge: Qwen3.5-9B for validity (VALIDITY: sound|unsound) and distinctness (0-100, accept < 95); post-hoc method classification by Claude Opus 5.5 on problems where both arms have >=1 correct completion (330 MATH, 130 Omni).

## Reproducibility on our hardware
A Qwen3.5-2B LoRA GRPO run with G=8 and 3072-token rollouts fits in 64 GB HBM on one MI210 in principle; the costly part is rollout generation and the 16-sample mining plus a 9B judge per routed prompt. The ROCm-6.2/gfx90a RL stack is a build cost, not a limit. CPU: no. Reproducing the paper's numbers would not tell us anything about our served models (not 2B, not math), so this is a decline on consumer grounds (A1).

## pass@k + dispersion metric for evals
Formula (1) in the paper is the standard unbiased estimator 1 - C(G-c,k)/C(G,k). The eval tower currently has no repeat policy; eval-tower-program.md:L971-L995 @ ac1347f records a MONITOR verdict for Pass^k keyed to an agentic eval with persisted trajectory, and says to reuse `_consistency_and_passk` rather than write a second estimator. Dispersion (mean pairwise cosine distance of correct completions, local Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B) is genuinely new relative to that note and could ride with the same trigger.

## Adversarial pass
Single group (Caltech), v1, no independent replication found. Objections: (1) checkpoint selection on the test set inflates the Omni peak; (2) one seed per arm; (3) the diversity judge trains and evaluates on similar signals (reward-hacking risk the paper itself attributes to Upskill); (4) the 'surface variance vs method variance' claim rests on one LLM judge; (5) pass@16 on Omni-MATH of 41-46% from a 2B model with 400 problems has CI width of roughly +-5 pp, comparable to the headline delta (the paper's CIs are shown in figures only).
