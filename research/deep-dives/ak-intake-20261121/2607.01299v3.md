## Settles
Exact or approximate? Composition S=T_C S_prev + S_C|0 is exact given identical layer inputs (rel L2 1.55e-15 FP64, layer 0) but approximate at depth: 8.92% rel L2 / 5.11 deg mean drift on Qwen3.5-35B-A3B (Table 4; FA layers disabled for the probe). Models: Qwen3.5-35B-A3B and 122B-A10B (GDN, dense transition), Ring-mini/flash (scalar). Qwen3.6/3.8 not tested. Quality: within +0.47/-0.56 points on Qwen3.5 (Ring -3.3/-3.4), 1.71 avg over 16 cells, F1/ROUGE only; Naive Addition loses 66.9%. TTFT 2.9-4.7x vs full, 2.8-4.1x vs prefix cache (8xH20, prewarmed); 1.49-1.85x QPS at 1 s SLO.
