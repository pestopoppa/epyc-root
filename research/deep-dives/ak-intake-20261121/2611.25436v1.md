## Grounding

The MI210 cost model:
- `program.md:L704-708` (research `3ab8362e5`): gain = (1+a)/(1+dW·s/R) - 1.
- s = 2.39 ms/row on a 52.5 ms chain round (`speculative-decoding.md:L1840`, `:L1980 @ ac1347f`).
- After the DTREE-ROUND-OVERHEAD fixes, only static greedy G12 KEEPs: G12/L 1.0266 [1.0100, 1.0432]. G16 0.997 and T16 0.959 do not (`:L1975`).
- The DTREE-TREE-QUALITY projection puts a cost-aware per-round W at 118.3 vs static greedy W12 at 118.0 tok/s (`:L981`). So an adaptive width controller has about +0.3% headroom on the current curve.

Raptor's budget starts at N=30, which is about 22 rows more than the chain. That costs roughly 40-50 ms on a 49-52 ms round, so Raptor's controller cannot beat static G12 under our s.

What does transfer:
- The frontier-gap predictor, a candidate for SPEC-CONF.1 (`:L616`).
- The recovery schedule, for CAFE-3 (`:L508`).

AK-H-MTP-DEGEN-1:
- It is **absent from every lane handoff**. Searched `handoffs/`, `research/` and `docs/` in the lane worktree.
- It exists only as research seed `scripts/kernel_rnd/autokernel/loop/seeds/hypotheses/18-mtp-degenerate-draft-suspend-20261120.md`.
- Raptor's rule maps onto it directly. Keep an EMA of slot acceptance. On sustained shallow rejection, cap the draft depth at 0-1. Relax by +1 on each fully accepted round. This is non-absorbing by construction.
