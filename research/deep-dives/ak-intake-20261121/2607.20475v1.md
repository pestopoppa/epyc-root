Other facts:
- Packed key = 16-bit order-preserving bf16 encoding (MSB toggle / complement) in the upper half and the inverted tile-local index in the lower half (uint32). Ties go to the lower index.
- Bitonic stage: kappa = log2 k = 7 merge stages over B_N, then fold to k in registers. CAS is written as min/max+select so it compiles to IMNMX.
- Stage 2 merges alternating-order sub-tiles without re-sorting (App D).
- Min-p in logit space (eq. 12): x_i >= max + ln(theta).
- FlashInfer was modified to take pre-drawn Gumbel noise as an input so it could be graph-captured.
- Code is "TBA" (not released).

## Adversarial pass

- WebSearch "SonicSampler tile-aware sampling kernels Together AI top-k benchmark" returned only the arXiv abstract (a restatement). It found no independent replication or criticism, and no code.
- Internal weaknesses:
  - The 16x is against a modified FlashInfer that is known to degrade with top-k.
  - The e2e baseline is a TRT-LLM that the authors extended themselves.
  - All numbers are from figures, with no absolute-latency table.
  - Tables 1 and 3 undercut the adaptive path for small batches.
