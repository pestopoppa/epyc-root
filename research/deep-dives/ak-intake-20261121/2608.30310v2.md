## What is logged / replayed
Per token: FA key, value and the FA output hidden h_i. A hit with matched length m fetches FA KV for all m tokens and only the last ceil(r*m) hiddens. Each linear group (an FA layer plus the following linear layers) runs the GDN recurrence S_i = T_i S_{i-1} + beta_i v_i k_i^T from zero over that tail; k, v, g (alpha), beta are recomputed inside the layers from the hidden. The group's last-layer FFN is skipped (not needed for state). The FA layers are not replayed.

## Quality-loss locations
Table 3 (App A, r=5% / full): OLMo LB-qasper@16K 0.308/0.394 (78%), musique@16K 0.282/0.305, RULER qa1@16K 0.645/0.725 (89%), cwe@8K 0.786/0.865 (91%), niah@8K 0.904/0.983 (92%); Qwen3.6-27B qasper@16K 0.481/0.521 (92%); Qwen3.5-4B musique@16K 0.422/0.439. No 24K-32K RULER variable-tracking cells exist in this paper, so FrontierReplay's 13-17% claim is neither confirmed nor refuted here.

## TTFT
Table 2 (mean of 20 reps; H2D-OVL+skip): at ~32K 108.8 / 86.2 / 251.8 ms vs full 1068.3 / 786.4 / 3605.2 ms (9.8x/9.1x/14.3x); at 8K only 2.5-5.7x. 10% replay lowers 27B 32K to 9.7x. The comparator is the no-cache prefill; there is no comparison with an exact checkpoint restore, whose TTFT would be far lower than 14x at matched prefixes.

## Adversarial pass
No independent replication found; it is a workshop note by the same group as SuffixReplay and LinearKV. Objections: speedup baseline is full prefill; averaged scores mask per-cell drops; unvalidated forgetting assumption; model-dependent (OLMo). FrontierReplay (intake-2758) reports bigger loss for a replay variant on Qwen3.5-9B VT at 24-32K.
