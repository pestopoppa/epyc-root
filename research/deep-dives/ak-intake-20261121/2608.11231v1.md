## Settles
Is PIC for GDN hybrids exact? NO. Both exact state composition and the single-state shortcut are approximations; the paper itself says exact algebra over isolated-chunk operators "need not reproduce the true state". Qwen3.6-27B (GDN, 48 GDN + 16 FA) at 20% recompute: LongBench QA avg 0.426-0.473 vs 0.551 full (77-86%); RULER-8K 0.738-0.829 vs 0.983 (75-84%); RULER-32K 0.567-0.829 vs 0.980. Summarization ROUGE-L 0.259-0.266 vs 0.274 (compressed spread). No Qwen3.5 tested; OLMo-Hybrid-7B and Granite-4.0-H-Tiny (Mamba-2) are the others.
