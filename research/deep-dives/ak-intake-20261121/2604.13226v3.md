## Settles
new entry. Answers the HyPIC dive question: KV Packet does not apply to GDN/recurrent layers as published (no recurrent-state story: header/trailer tokens change a document recurrent state but the state cannot be concatenated without a (T,S) compose). It is a competing approach to EPIC/CacheBlend-style selective recompute (the KPF-62 family), not to HyPIC.

## Key claims (corrected)
0. Approximate, trained, attention-only: cached per-document KV gets 8 trainable header + 8 trainable trailer soft tokens (float32 adapters, few KB) distilled against the frozen model with KL on its own full-context generations; at serve time only RoPE re-rotation + concatenation, so online FLOPs ~ 0 and TTFT ~ No-Recompute.
1. Quality is partial recovery, not parity: vs Full Recompute on Llama-3.2-3B recovery is 91.3% (Biography), 92.4% (NIAH), 77.8% (HotpotQA), 47.9% (MuSiQue), mean 77.4%; on Llama-3.1-8B HotpotQA macro F1 0.423 vs 0.460 full (micro 0.382 vs 0.408); NIAH recovery falls 91.5% (8k/4 packets) to 76.5% (32k/16 packets); long-form summarization loses 66.5% order-balanced pairwise judgments.
2. Models: Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen-3-4B (main), Llama-3.2-3B (KVLink comparison); a single A100-80GB, all pure-attention. No hybrid/GDN/recurrent model is evaluated, and the paper lists limited model-family coverage as a limitation.
3. Mechanism claim: the damage in naive concatenation is mostly boundary/attention-sink artifacts not cross-document attention loss; a Full-Attention-with-adapters arm (0.428 micro F1) matches full recompute (0.408), and headers/trailers absorb the sink mass (Fig 5).

- KPF-62 is the selective-recompute family (EPIC/CacheBlend/CacheClip class): content-keyed store, K re-rotation, selector (head window W=64 + check-layer K deviation), sparse recompute. KV Packet is the recompute-free alternative to that family. Slice 1 (kv-cache-program.md:584) already has RoPE re-rotation and a startup round-trip self-check; `qwen35` (hybrid) refuses cleanly there.
- Hybrid/GDN: KV Packet has no recurrent-state mechanism; HyPIC's (T_C,S) compose is the only in-lane route, scoped in KPF-62-P3-SKELETON.

## Adversarial pass
KVShareArena (search snippet, 2609.10266) reports soft-token-adapter reuse dropping significantly in four of six cells (up to -.135) when the cache comes from a different producer checkpoint; the KV Packet repo (github.com/ChuangtaoChen-TUM/KVPacket) exists with a library branch and a paper-artifact branch (search snippet; not cloned). No peer review found. Objections from the paper's own limitations: adapters assume corpus aligned with training distribution; long-context recovery falls to 76.5% at 32k/16 packets with 128k untested; long-form generation loses 66.5% of order-balanced pairwise judgments; the paper states it has not run a header-vs-trailer ablation and that its KVLink comparison does not isolate supervision or placement. Headline F1/FLOPs/TTFT curves live in Fig 3 (image), so the 19.45x NIAH TTFT is per the text only.

## Reader should conclude
Cite KV Packet for: boundary-artifact/attention-sink evidence (Fig 5, Table 6) that supports the head-window recompute in KPF-62.3, and as the recompute-free trained-adapter baseline. Do NOT cite it for hybrid/GDN reuse, as lossless/exact, or as a plug-in for orchestrator RAG/tool-output reuse: it needs a trained adapter per model and domain (30 epochs, 256-512 samples, A100), recovers 77% on average and 48% on multi-hop with one doc per packet, and an independent benchmark (KVShareArena, via search) reports drops when the cache comes from a different checkpoint.
