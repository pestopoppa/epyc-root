## Settles (what the KV Packet dive asked)
The KV Packet dive carried this from a search snippet as "adapter reuse dropping in four of six cells, up to -.135". Full text: KV Packet's trained adapter falls significantly below its same-producer score in **12 of 18** pair-dataset tests (six producer pairs x three QA subsets), worst **-.146 F1**; LegoLink without recompute loses up to .151 (8/18); every other repair (CacheBlend, LegoLink k=2, APE, Block-Attention, all compression) loses under .02 F1 on average and at most .04 in any test. Retraining the adapter per producer removes the loss (about 20 GPU-hours per producer, Qwen3-8B receiver). So the drop is specific to the trained adapter, not a property of KV reuse in general.

What it measures: a 2x2 design (same or different preceding text x same or different producer weights), receiver and input text fixed; quality as PGR (0 = no documents, 1 = full recompute over the same text), plus compute, KV memory and TTFT. Data: Qasper, MultiFieldQA, HotpotQA (2,150 questions), token-F1. Models: Qwen3-4B/8B/14B, Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507, Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, all full attention; six producer pairs (Table 16: base predecessor or sibling, e.g. Qwen3-8B read from Qwen3-8B-Base and from DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B). **No hybrid, GDN or SSM model.** "Quant. 4-bit" is KV-cache quantisation on the receiver, not a quantised-weights producer.

## Grounding (lane @ 4b428f1)
- Prefix cache / chunk-KV (KPF-62, kv-cache-program.md L581-L584): attention-only slice 1; hybrid/recurrent/SWA refuse in libllama export. KVShareArena's models are exactly the attention-only regime, so it neither supports nor contradicts the hybrid PIC path; it does support that an approximate gate must be run per served model (receiver-specific).
- Champion rebuilds (slot save/restore, --cache-ram): a rebuild keeps the weights; the paper varies weights, not kernels or builds. It therefore does not bear on build-to-build restore. Restore safety across builds rests on the existing receipts that bind model digests (kv-cache-program.md L196-L198) and on `--cache-ram` storing whole-slot states by exact prefix (DESIGN.md L35). It would bear only if a checkpoint were restored under different weights (new quant, fine-tune, drafter swap): then expect a measurable quality cost (Table 17) and refuse by key.
- Approximate PIC gates: no new gate. Its "12/18" is a statement about a trained adapter we do not use (KV Packet declined, intake-2782).
