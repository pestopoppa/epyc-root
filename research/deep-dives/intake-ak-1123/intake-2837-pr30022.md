## 2. Key claims

0. **Origin of the rule: the force-tiling predicate is introduced here, GCN-only. CONFIRMED.** Three triggers, any one forces plain tiling instead of stream-k: last tile wave at least 90% full, tile count at least twice the stream-k grid, or `k_chunks <= occupancy * tiles_nwaves`. The "grid" in the second trigger is `nsm * occupancy` on GCN and `nsm` elsewhere. The rule is implemented as a new template parameter `force_tiling` on `mul_mat_q` that routes the kernel through its existing tiling branch.
   - Anchors (diff): `const bool force_tiling = GGML_CUDA_CC_IS_GCN(cc) &&`; `(tiles_efficiency_percent >= 90 || ntiles_dst >= 2*stream_k_blocks ||`; `k_chunks <= config.occupancy * tiles_nwaves);`; `const int stream_k_blocks = GGML_CUDA_CC_IS_GCN(cc) ? nsm * config.occupancy : nsm;`
