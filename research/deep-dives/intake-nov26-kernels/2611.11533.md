# Ostinato: Low-Latency CPU-GPU MoE Inference for Local Agents

v2 improves evaluation specificity. 2.5–6.6× median tool-turn wait reduction, 1.36–3.83× session speedup default and 1.30–1.94× aligned at 512-token threshold and matched loading. Long-prefill scheduler deduplicates transfers across routed rows for each cold expert, leaving the static resident pool in place. CPU/GPU split and all-zero graph slots are different levers. Tested 4090D × 1–4 with dual Xeon DDR4 86–88 GB/s host read.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11533v2)

## Evidence checked

- During short tool turns, Ostinato computes resident expert routes on GPUs and sparse cold routes on CPU without copying expert weights. (Abstract and §3).
- Ostinato deduplicates cold expert uploads on long prefills. (Abstract and §3).
- The authors report 1.30–1.94× faster session completion when the baselines match Ostinato’s upload threshold and weight-loading settings. (Abstract; §4 setup).
- Ostinato uses all-zero expert slots and device-side synchronization to capture each GPU’s multi-GPU decode step in one CUDA graph. (Abstract and §3.4).

## Counterevidence and limits

Tideway emphasizes phase-aware tier reallocation and expert migration, a real alternative. Aligned and stitched baselines narrow the claimed benefit; short-turn replay locality and tuned threshold may not transfer to another workload.
