## Evidence checked

- Any-Rate restricts selected bit planes to extended Hamming codewords in blocks of eight weights, encoding those planes at 0.5 bits per weight. (Abstract).
- The Any-Rate kernel keeps the mode lookup table for each row in shared memory. (Appendix H).
- In batch-1 Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct decode on an RTX 5070 Ti, the authors report Any-Rate within 3% of Any-Precision throughput at both 3 and 4 bits. (§5, Table 3).

## Counterevidence and limits

Any-Precision LLM predates the checkpoint-overlay idea and is a strong integer baseline. Table 3 at 2–4 bits does not favor Any-Rate at every rate; RTX consumer CUDA and modeled LPDDR efficiency do not establish gfx90a or Zen5 benefit.

## Derived action for review

On an eligible DS41 or Q38FN low-bit lane, use real expert-tensor shapes to compare normalized format bytes, arithmetic correctness and decode/prefill latency to incumbent GGUF, then equal-bytes quality before funding a model port. Keep precision switching a distinct experiment.
