## Evidence checked

- Theorem 1 establishes exact target-distribution sampling for the AsymSpec rejection-sampling algorithm. (§1, contributions; Theorem 1).
- Across reasoning, code generation and summarization tasks, the authors report speedups ranging from 2.29× to 4.95× over autoregressive decoding. (Abstract).
- In the reported prompt-length ablation, acceptance metrics continued improving beyond 1,024 prompt tokens while throughput degraded. (§4.5).
