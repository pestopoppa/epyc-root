## Evidence checked

- For batch-invariant probability and logit sampling, the PR says a draw is determined by the source row’s values and the seed and offset supplied for its output row. (PR Description, opening paragraph).
- The PR describes the RNG streams of batch-invariant and default sampling as incompatible. (PR Description, second paragraph).
- In the B200 one-row, 32,768-entry sampling_from_probs benchmark, the PR reports median kernel times of 16.2 µs in batch-invariant mode and 4.5 µs for the default path, using BF16 and FP32 inputs respectively. (PR Performance, B200 sampling_from_probs N=32768 M=1).

## Counterevidence and limits

FlashInfer's ReproSpec RFC (#733) and current sampler docs distinguish same-batch determinism from per-request batch invariance. EPYC's SPEC-RNG.1 retest had logit drift at or before every differing CPU token and its MI210 serving check measured zero net same-seed benefit across 48 pairs. No independent MI210/Zen5 performance replication of this CUDA source surfaced.
