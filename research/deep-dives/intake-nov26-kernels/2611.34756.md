## Evidence checked

- CARE-OPD uses student-seeded teacher completions and response-level confidence gating during collection, then selects replay turns using downstream execution evidence. (Abstract, method in §§3.3–3.4).
- On SWE-bench Verified, the authors report 59.5% task success for CARE-OPD, 45% less end-to-end training time and 44% fewer environment actions than asynchronous one-turn online OPD. (Abstract, one-turn comparator specified in §4.3 Table 2).
- In the SWE-bench Verified replay-allocation ablation, hindsight selection improved task success by 3.6 percentage points over each of uniform replay and a size-matched random subset. (§4.5 controlled replay allocation, Table 4 discussion).
- The authors say replayed responses were not executed and that their confidence measure reflects teacher distributional certainty rather than response correctness. (§5 Limitations).
