# DFly2: Turbocharging Long-Context Speculative Decoding by Sharing the Target's Sparsity

Three-layer 128-expert drafters have 0.4–1.1B active parameters. DS-V4.1-Flash attended set includes 512 selected positions, a 128-token window and six block rows. Prior indices overlap almost 90% in one DS target; that is coverage, not acceptance. Acceptance gain 3.9–10.5% general, 10.0–31.5% long/agentic vs stronger same-recipe baseline. H20 serving gains are matched per-user speed at high concurrency.

[Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34776v1)

## Evidence checked

- DFly2 reads paired target-layer KV at positions selected by that layer’s indexer and maintains no independent KV cache or scoring pass. (Abstract).
- The authors trained DFlash, DSpark and window-matched DFlash with the same recipe, dataset and number of steps as DFly2. (§5, setup).
- For greedy DS-V4.1-Flash serving at matched per-user decode speed, the authors report 62% and 81% higher throughput than DSpark on CodeSpan 64K and SWE 64K, respectively. (Abstract).
