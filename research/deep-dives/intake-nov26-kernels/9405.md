GDN fp32 state commits every 64 rows with BF16 and open-chunk scratch per slot, reproducing prefill arithmetic in decode and spans. Prefill ratios involve slot-index readback and cold-L2 benchmarks. Output_final_state returns the boundary pool. Duplicate decode/span slots are undefined and replacing a slot does not have KDA detection.
