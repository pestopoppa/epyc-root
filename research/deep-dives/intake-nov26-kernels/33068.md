# llama : share compute buffers with the partner context

Draft, head 9c7c5f8c66de2b8245ac124a930c0587cc0bebd8. The partner must be serial, scheduler backends and buffer types match, and exactly one non-CPU device must register as CUDA. ~130 MiB MTP and ~190 MiB DFlash2 on each RTX 5090 and RTX PRO 6000 in the reported 20-block setup.

[Primary source](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/33068)

## Evidence checked

- The draft shares a partner context’s compute buffers through ggml_backend_sched_share_buffers. (PR overview, partner scheduler wiring).
- Buffer sharing requires one sequence in each context, the same single CUDA device and no pipeline parallelism. (PR additional information, eligibility).
- The PR reports about 130 MiB less GPU memory for MTP and 190 MiB less for DFlash2 on both an RTX 5090 and an RTX PRO 6000 in its 20-block setup. (PR additional information, benchmark).
