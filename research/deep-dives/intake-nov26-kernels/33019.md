# CUDA: split gate and up weight reads for small-k MMVQ

Open, head 8d9e8f29990360ac1ee43e7863ce9df4bf7a519c. Fused small-K MMVQ gains a separate gate loop, while the cutoff admits exact-fit K. RTX3060 tg128 randomized median of 4 runs with 3 reps: +21.87% Qwen2.5 3B Q3_K_M, +13.31% IQ4_XS, +5.07% OLMoE 1B–7B Q4_K_M; Q8_0 and Qwen3 8B Q4_K_M near zero.

[Primary source](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/pull/33019)

## Evidence checked

- The patch splits gate and up weight reads into separate loops when the CUDA MMVQ small_k condition holds. (PR overview, first paragraph).
- The PR reports llama-bench tg128 results on an RTX 3060 using CUDA 13.5. (PR additional information, benchmark setup).
- The PR reports a 2% performance decrease from changing the small_k threshold alone. (PR overview, threshold paragraph).
