# Training & Distillation

**Category**: `training_distillation`
**Confidence**: verified
**Last compiled**: 2026-07-28
**Sources**: 36 documents

## Summary

Training and distillation research for the EPYC stack focuses on three pillars: (1) improving worker model quality through contrastive training techniques like ACT, (2) compressing reasoning output through self-distillation methods like OPSDC, and (3) the SkillBank experience distillation system that converts raw episodic trajectories into structured, reusable behavioral principles.

The PostTrainBench study (intake-105/106) established that autonomous agent-driven post-training can beat official model instruction tuning on narrow tasks (BFCL +22pp on Gemma-3-4B) but achieves less than half the performance on general benchmarks. A critical finding is that medium reasoning effort outperforms high reasoning effort for strong models -- over-deliberation wastes budget on planning rather than experimentation. The 5-hour training plateau observed is specifically an SFT plateau; RL-based methods like ACT demonstrate gains beyond it.

ACT (Agentic Critical Training) introduces contrastive action discrimination where a model learns WHY certain actions are better, not just that they are. Trained via GRPO with no reasoning supervision, ACT produces +5pp gains over both imitation learning and RL baselines while preserving general reasoning (unlike IL which causes -6.91pp catastrophic forgetting on GPQA-Diamond). Cross-size transfer is validated: a 4B model trained on 8B trajectories matches or exceeds the 8B on out-of-distribution tasks.

The SkillBank system (Ch.15) operationalizes distillation in production. Three teacher models (Claude Opus 4.6, gpt-5.3-codex, Qwen3-235B local) distill raw trajectories into structured skills stored in SQLite + FAISS. Two-level retrieval injects skills into prompts at under 2ms latency with 5-15x token compression over raw trajectories. Recursive skill evolution promotes effective skills and deprecates failing ones. The system is gated behind the `ORCHESTRATOR_SKILLBANK=1` feature flag with 139 tests and graceful degradation.

SEAL control vectors for concise reasoning (seal-concise-reasoning experiment) showed mixed results: MoE models responded well (-7.5% tokens, no accuracy loss), dense models were neutral, and SSM-hybrid models (Qwen3.5-35B-A3B) suffered catastrophic failure (generation collapsed to 1 token). The experiment was parked in favor of AM KV compaction which delivers 5x compression at zero degradation.

A critical finding from SFT generalization research (intake-374, intake-378) is that **reasoning pattern structure determines SFT generalization quality more than quantity, loss, or data diversity**. DeepSeek-R1's divergent, branch-heavy traces produce 21pp worse generalization than gpt-oss-120b's convergent, deductive traces on Llama3.1-8B -- despite lower training loss. The "dilution effect" means low SFT loss is a misleading quality signal: R1's lower loss comes from trivially learned routine tokens, not better reasoning. Filtering branch-heavy trajectories by branching keyword proportion (Proxy 2) yields +3.6pp average improvement at zero cost. Independently, repeated exposure dominates coverage: 128 epochs on 400 samples beats 1 epoch on 51,200 by 12-26pp on AIME'24/25 (confirmed across two independent studies).

Aletheia RLVR (intake-370) provides scale-dependent training recipes for verification models: 1.5B models need on-policy GRPO with negative samples but can skip thinking traces; 14B models require thinking traces and negative samples for stability; DPO is catastrophic at 1.5B scale (-23.4%) but viable at 14B with Easy-to-Hard data. Training is GPU-only (16 rollouts/step), making the 1.5B scale the sweet spot for CPU inference verification with training deferred to DGX Spark.

The 2026-07-02 cross-model LoRA-transfer / hypernetwork-adapter cluster (intake-764..771 + existing intake-098/707/618) unifies eight works around one operational value proposition — adapt a frozen LLM cheaply, then carry that adaptation across a rotating open-weight roster without full re-training per base. The cluster shares one blocker (adapter/hypernetwork/converter training is gradient-based and GPU-gated, the same Phase-B gate that backbones the doc-to-LoRA line), with two exceptions cutting through: Cross-LoRA (intake-765) is the only CPU-plausible member (pure SVD + least-squares weight-space port, no training, but only ~14% per-task lift recovery), and Text-to-LoRA (intake-771, Sakana, ICML'25) is the only member shipping open code AND open pretrained hypernetwork weights — enabling a genuinely zero-cost CPU validation spike. The Platonic Representation Hypothesis (intake-770) is the cited theoretical basis, but its Capacity corollary is the load-bearing caveat for us: representation convergence is strongest for large/capable models and weakest for small/heterogeneous ones — exactly the regime our small drafters and mixed roster occupy. Do not assume portability; local re-measurement gates any adoption.

## Key Findings

### New (2026-07-28, looped-transformer / recurrent-depth lineage — intake-902..912, 7 Stage-2 dives)

- **The lineage map is now dive-verified end-to-end**: Universal Transformers (intake-902, superseded root) → MoEUT shared-layer MoE (intake-903) → Relaxed Recursive Transformers (intake-904, ICLR 2025) → Huginn depth-recurrence (intake-905, NeurIPS 2025 spotlight) → Mixture-of-Recursions (intake-906, NeurIPS 2025) → DeepLoop loop-aware normalization (intake-907) / Loopie layer-loop MoE (intake-908). Four distinct **loop placements**: model-loop (Ouro), layer-loop R=2 (Loopie), token-routed recursion (MoR), converted recurrence (Retrofitted Recurrence, intake-911). All share one gate: no llama.cpp/GGUF path exists for any of them.
- **Naive layer sharing loses; every viable design restores capacity another way** — MoEUT via fine-grained MoE experts in the shared layer (C4 PPL wins of 0.13–0.25 at ≥244M, small and from a ~6.5B-token regime), RRT via SVD-initialized layer-wise LoRA (+up to 6.5pp over zero-init). Follow-up work (Mixture-of-LoRAs, 2512.12880) already contests RRT's static-LoRA choice at BERT scale.
- **Conversion beats from-scratch, but is not cheap**: RRT's pretrained-weight init and intake-911's recurrence curriculum (GSM8K 26.5→51.2 over r=1→32 on converted TinyLlama) both verify, but conversion costs 26–52B uptraining tokens on cluster hardware, and the converted model is *worse* than a non-recurrent post-trained control at r=1 (26.5 vs 45.2) — the test-time knob only buys back quality at ~32× core FLOPs.
- **Huginn's "50B-parameter equivalent" is FLOPs-unrolling arithmetic** (num_steps × 1.5B + 2B), not parity with any 50B model — absolute MMLU is 31.38 vs OLMo-7B's 60.56. Its four zero-shot inference mechanisms are unevenly evidenced: per-token KL-exit and cyclic KV-cache sharing are quantified (KV budget 4 of 32 steps with **no** MTBench loss), continuous-CoT is weak (~1–2 steps), and self-speculative decoding has **zero published numbers**.
- **Recurrence depth is not a CoT substitute at 3.5B** (intake-910 probe, verified figures): CoT-suppressed GSM8K rises only 3.11→4.93 strict from r=4→32 and plateaus, vs 24.87/38.13 for the same model with explicit CoT. The probe does not refute latent computation wholesale (lens methods only detect token-decodable content — authors concede this) and does not touch the compute-scaling claim.
- **No throughput number in this family is a serving number**: RRT's 2.66× is an oracle-exit *simulation* with unspecified hardware; MoR's 2.06× is a bespoke H100 depth-wise-batching harness with no prefill workload; MoR also *loses* to vanilla under isoFLOPs at 135M (recursive capacity bottleneck) — its Pareto claim holds at ≥360M only.
- **Loopie is the credibility cautionary tale of the batch** (dive-overturned): its claimed HF preview weights and vllm/megatron code links were all dead 11 days post-publication; its v1 abstract-level IMO/IPhO gold claims (GPT-5.5 LLM-as-judge self-scoring landing exactly on the 35/35 gold threshold) were silently removed in v2 three days later while newsletters still restate them; its "compute-matched" crossover is wall-clock-matched on the authors' own Megatron stack vs their own 800B-token baseline reproduction, explicitly not FLOP-matched and explicitly not established for inference.
- **EPYC posture: watch-only, with corroboration banked** — the family independently confirms the TIDE post-mortem (competitive early exit/adaptive depth was only ever demonstrated with training-time loop/exit losses; no paper ran a post-hoc control). Watch triggers recorded in `reasoning-compression.md`: conversion recipe ≤5B tokens or single-GPU scale, a ≥7B instruct-tuned looped checkpoint, or upstream GGUF/llama.cpp looped-model support.

All cluster fidelity figures are self-reported preprints (only LoRA-X and Text-to-LoRA peer-reviewed; PorTAL is a product-lab writeup) — hypothesis-shaping, never decision-gating per MEASUREMENT.md. ### New (2026-06-20, evolution-aware code adapters + CPU-trainable reward/coder recipes)

- **The CPU-relevant blocker for hypernetwork-generated adapters is orchestrator LoRA *wiring*, not adapter hot-swap.** A recurring conflation is that doc/code-to-LoRA adapters can't be served on this stack because hot-swap is missing. That is wrong: llama.cpp LoRA hot-swap is fully implemented on our fork -- load-at-startup, per-request adapter override, aLoRA auto-activate, and selective KV-cache clearing (08-doc-to-lora Finding 1, verified). The actual gap is the orchestrator wiring (08-doc-to-lora Finding 7, verified): no `--lora` in `build_server_command()`, no `lora_path`/`lora_adapters` in `ModelConfig`, no `"lora"` field in `_build_payload()`. Any serving-layer follow-up attaches to the existing LoRAX/S-LoRA serving note, not to a multi-file-coding-completion capability. Hypernetwork *training* stays GPU-gated. Sources: [08-doc-to-lora-prototype.md](../handoffs/completed/08-doc-to-lora-prototype.md) Findings 1+7, [08-doc-to-lora Code2LoRA addendum 2026-06-20](../handoffs/completed/08-doc-to-lora-prototype.md).
- **Code2LoRA extends the hypernetwork-to-LoRA line to *evolving* codebases via a recurrent diff-tracking state -- the one genuinely-new mechanism vs already-ingested DocToLoRA** [intake-707, arXiv:2606.06492, external/preprint]. The static variant (Code2LoRA-Static, one adapter per repo snapshot) merely reproduces DocToLoRA's known hypernetwork->adapter recipe and matches the per-repository-LoRA upper bound (63.8% cross-repo / 66.2% in-repo assertion-completion EM). The net-new contribution is **Code2LoRA-Evo**: a GRU hidden state updated *per code diff* that amortizes per-commit adapter maintenance into a recurrent state, hitting 60.3% cross-repo EM (+5.2pp over a single shared LoRA). This is time-aware adapter specialization -- a candidate for keeping a coder-role adapter fresh as a repo changes without regenerating from scratch. On reopen of 08-doc-to-lora (GPU + REPL-uncovered need), evaluate the Evo/GRU variant starting from Finding 5 (architecture compatibility) onward, not from Phase A. Source: [intake-707](../research/intake_index.yaml).
- **Code2LoRA ships RepoPeftBench -- a static+evolution evaluation harness with commit-derived tasks** [intake-707, external/preprint]. 604 Python repos: static split 40K/12K tasks, evolution split 215K/87K commit-derived tasks. This is the first benchmark in our corpus that scores adapter quality *under software evolution* (commit-by-commit), distinct from one-shot SQuAD/DROP/LongBench that DocToLoRA used. Relevant if a per-repo coder adapter ever becomes load-bearing -- it provides an externally-defined, evolution-aware fit metric rather than a static snapshot score. Source: [intake-707](../research/intake_index.yaml).
- **A tiny pointwise reference-grounded MSE reward model is CPU-trainable today and is the distillation-training datapoint to mine, not a Bradley-Terry ranker** [AVB offline reward stack, intake-706/716/717/719, external/practitioner-self-reported]. The recipe: a small sentence-transformer encoder (`all-MiniLM-L6-v2` ~22M or `distilbert-base-cased` ~66M), input formatted as `"{reference} [SEP] {response}"`, meanmax pooling (concat mean+max) -> dropout -> single linear scalar head, trained with **MSE** against a 1-5 rubric label normalized to [0,1], early-stopping on validation MSE. Two-stage finetune: encoder **frozen first, then last 3 layers unfrozen** with **differential learning rates** (head 5e-4, encoder 5e-5). Augmentation: contrastive minimal-edit pairs + synthetic-confound decoys for surface-similarity robustness. The entire stack -- training and serving -- runs on CPU, which is what makes it relevant to this hardware (no TRL/RLHF framework, plain PyTorch). All magnitude numbers (~0.72/0.76 Spearman, ~0.93 answer-equivalence) are self-reported observations, never decision-gating. Source: [AVB offline reward stack deep dive](../research/deep-dives/2026-06-20-avb-offline-reward-stack.md). (Primary home is cost-aware-routing; the *training-recipe* angle is logged here.)
- **The published AVB checkpoint is paraphrase-blind, which is the load-bearing failure mode for any quality-oracle training target** [intake-719, external/practitioner]. `neuraltxt-reward-tiny` (22M MiniLM, 0-1 score) self-reports good confound resistance (~6% fooled) but catches only **~3% of synonym swaps** -- a correct-but-reworded answer is frequently scored low. Before reusing this recipe to *train* a graded label for distillation/verifier targets, a paraphrase-correct vs verbatim-correct stress test is mandatory; a paraphrase-blind oracle injects systematic noise against verbose/reworded specialist outputs and is worse than an honest binary ladder. Dataset provenance (intake-717: 22,423 rows, undocumented judge/rubric) must be inspected before trusting `orig_score` as a clean training target. Source: [AVB offline reward stack deep dive](../research/deep-dives/2026-06-20-avb-offline-reward-stack.md).
- **Execution-verified, test-gated chain-of-thought is a concrete CPU-deployable distillation recipe instance** [intake-702, external/community fine-tune, credibility null]. A community gemma-4-12B coder fine-tune (`gemma-4-12B-coder`, base `google/gemma-4-12B-it`, Apache 2.0) distills from two CoT sources where **every training trace is execution-verified before ingestion**: Composer 2.5 traces are kept only when the solution passed tests, and Fable 5 re-derives self-consistent CoT for Composer-2.5 failures (also test-gated). This is rejection-sampling distillation with a test-pass filter -- no synthetic reasoning without test validation -- a clean datapoint for the "quality > quantity, verified traces" thread already established by Simula's double-critic rejection sampling and the SFT-generalization filtering findings. Caveat: the v1 card carries no coding benchmarks (only a v2 self-report that isn't this checkpoint), and a dense 12B reads ~12B params/token vs ~3B active for the MoE incumbent worker_general, so it is likely slower per-token on BW-bound CPU; gemma4_unified mainline support must be confirmed before any bench. Source: [intake-702](../research/intake_index.yaml).

### New (2026-04-22, DD4)

- **Post-training diversity collapse is STRUCTURAL (in weights), not a generation-format issue** [intake-441, arxiv:2604.16027]. OLMo 3 three-lineage study: Think models lose most semantic diversity at SFT stage; Instruct models see larger effects from DPO. CoT suppression preserves answer-level diversity in Think models — collapse is in the weights, not the generation format. **Inference-time interventions (conciseness prompts, temperature adjustment, reasoning-length alarms) cannot recover training-time diversity loss.** Decomposition: diversity loss = quality-control component + residual/genuine-narrowing component. Task-dependent. **Implication for EPYC**: (1) model selection must include diversity metrics (distinct-2, self-BLEU, entropy, TTR) alongside accuracy for any checkpoint swap — see `eval-tower-verification.md` EV-8; (2) PromptForge mutation diversity is fundamentally bounded by base-model diversity, so a diversity-coverage term is needed (autopilot AP-35); (3) SFT-dominant narrowing on reasoning tracks, DPO on chat tracks — training-pipeline choices have measurable downstream impact. Deep dive: `/workspace/research/deep-dives/diversity-collapse-posttraining.md`. **Tier 2b amended 2026-04-22 (Verbalized Sampling, arXiv 2510.01171)**: load-bearing "inference-irrecoverable" claim is refuted — a distributional-sampling prompt recovers 66.8% of the base-model diversity gap. EV-8 amended to multi-signal gate (Tier 1 WARN / Tier 2 REJECT only when distinct-2 drop > 20% AND semantic-embedding-agreement drop > 10% AND quality not up AND VS-recovery < 50%). **Scaffolding landed 2026-04-22 (NIB2-42)**: `src/safety_gate.py` (EvalResult + SafetyGate, NaN-safe, warn-only default), `src/tools/diversity/metrics.py` (entropy/distinct-n/self-BLEU/TTR/semantic-agreement), `src/tools/diversity/verbalized_sampling.py` (recovery probe). - **Simula provides a principled mechanism-design framework for synthetic data generation and evaluation.** Simula (arxiv:2603.29791, intake-410, TMLR 2026 with J2C certification) decomposes data generation into four independently controllable axes: coverage (taxonomy-based global diversification), local variation (meta-prompting), complexity (configurable complexification fraction), and quality (double-critic rejection sampling). Key finding: there is no single optimal way to generate synthetic data -- the relationship between data properties and downstream performance is deeply idiosyncratic. High complexity yields +10% accuracy on GSM8k (strong teacher, 88%) but hurts on LEXam (weak teacher, 57%). The full system (all axes) almost always dominates simpler baselines despite costing 5x more inference per data point -- training costs dominate generation costs. Quality > Quantity: better data scales better. [simula-synthetic-data-generation.md](../research/deep-dives/simula-synthetic-data-generation.md)

The generator-critic refinement loop discovers edge cases humans missed (novelty score 0.94). Global diversification (deep taxonomy sampling) drives dataset-wide embedding diversity; local diversification (meta-prompting) drives within-cluster variation. The two are additive. [simula-synthetic-data-generation.md](../research/deep-dives/simula-synthetic-data-generation.md)

Beyond this point, more data yields diminishing returns regardless of quality. When teacher accuracy is low (LEXam 57%), the double-critic correctly rejects 61% of generated data -- the pipeline becomes extremely inefficient but produces correct behavior (not generating wrong training data). Unfiltered R1 traces produce 21pp worse generalization on Llama3.1-8B. Zero training cost -- preprocessing only.

Zero training cost -- uses local LLM for taxonomy generation and coverage scoring. Applicable to benchmark construction and specialist routing seeder.
- **Simula's complexity-aware insights for routing**: A model's optimal complexity band is bounded by its competence (teacher ceiling). For routing: high-complexity queries should route to strongest model, low-complexity to cheapest. Elo complexity scores could serve as routing classifier features.

## 2026 On-Policy-Distillation cluster — converging on token-selective objectives (2026-05-28)

**Maturing subfield, not collapsing.** Tier-2b expansion 2026-05-28 surfaced four concurrent 2026 OPD papers (`arxiv:2603.25562` Fu/CASIA · `arxiv:2604.13016` Li/Tsinghua "Rethinking OPD" → intake-644 · `arxiv:2605.11182` Zhu "Many Faces" · `arxiv:2605.10194` Wang TRACE → intake-645). None cite each other or the three existing OPD entries (intake-639 Apple "Unmasking OPD" · intake-642 Sang/Xu/Zhou TIP · intake-643 EffOPD) — independent parallel discovery. Convergent picture: naive sampled-token / all-token OPD on long reasoning trajectories is consistently broken (entropy spikes, length shortening, val drops, repetition loops); the fix family converges on **support-restriction** (top-K, overlap-mass, Soft-OR, per-token routing) and **distribution-shift stabilization** (cold-start SFT, RLVR-adapted teachers).

**Inference-side angles the initial intake missed.** Three of the family have downstream-checkpoint or logprobs-only artifacts deployable on our forward-only stack:
- **intake-639 (Apple, Unmasking OPD)**: diagnostic is logprobs-only (not gradient-access-requiring). Runs against any (student, teacher) checkpoint pair on our llama.cpp stack to screen third-party OPD-distilled releases. Relevance low → medium; credibility 5 → 6.
- **intake-644 (Tsinghua, Rethinking OPD)**: token-overlap-concentration metric (72%→91% overlap ratio during training; 97-99% of useful gradient mass on overlap tokens) is a single scalar computable from logprobs over teacher top-K — strictly simpler and more falsifiable than the gradient-alignment cosine. Two-stage cold-start-SFT-then-OPD recipe with 3K-7K response-length sweet spot. **Likely v1 token selector for any drafter we'd fund on cloud GPU**, over TIP Soft-OR.
- **intake-645 (TRACE)**: per-token tri-routing among {FKL, RKL, ∅(GRPO)}. **Scale-inversion** is the load-bearing finding — FKL dominates at 8B, RKL dominates at 1.7B. Anneal-to-GRPO schedule means trained model has **zero inference-time overhead**. For ≤2B drafters specifically, RKL-on-error variant is the v1 recipe candidate over Soft-OR.
- **intake-643 (EffOPD)**: SVD analysis shows OPD-trained 8B checkpoints have effective rank 2,341 vs 2,754 for RL-trained (top 1% captures 94.7%). Direct (un-tested by authors) corollary: **OPD-distilled models should LoRA-compress better than RL-trained ones** — evidence for the existing rank-8 default in Doc-to-LoRA Phase B ([`08-doc-to-lora-prototype.md`](../handoffs/active/08-doc-to-lora-prototype.md)). Verdict not_applicable → worth_investigating.

**For FastDraft drafter training** (intake-624): for a 14B→1.5B drafter on rented cloud GPU, the v1 recipe candidate is intake-644's two-stage (cold-start SFT + overlap-restricted OPD); for ≤2B targets specifically, intake-645's RKL-on-error TRACE variant may dominate.
