# EXL3 backlog — ROI ranking vs continuing DS41 AutoKernel

Date: 2026-10-03. Read-only investigation. Paths are relative to `/workspace` unless they are absolute.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **Nothing EXL3-related has ever run on an MI210, here or anywhere we have found.**
   - Our standalone gfx90a kernels compile and pass host checks but have zero device executions (`repos/epyc-inference-research/scripts/kernel_rnd/exl3_gfx90a/validation/status-20260926.json`, G3/G4/G5 "OPEN").
   - No EXL3 weights are on disk: `models/turboderp/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-exl3/` holds only `download.log`, after 180 GB was reclaimed (`handoffs/completed/exl3-trellis-cpu-kernel.md:5`).
   - The only EXL3 tensors on the host are three 128×128 fixture slices in the research repo: `scripts/kernel_rnd/exl3/fixtures/real-{mul1-k3,mul1-k4,mcg-k4}`, sourced from turboderp Qwen3.8-Flash-Next and 0xSero GLM-5.3-Flash.
2. **The kernels that exist are correctness references, not speed kernels.**
   - The wave64 GEMV is one lane per output column, with a serial loop that decodes every weight individually (`exl3_gfx90a/kernels.hip:80-91`).
   - The MFMA path decodes B one element at a time from global memory (`kernels.hip:97-114`).
   - **No INF-80 task covers performance-grade gfx90a kernels.** EXL3-7 ("measure local policy and performance") assumes they exist. This is the plan's largest hidden cost.
3. **On the dense production 27B, EXL3 on the MI210 is mainly a VRAM lever, with an uncertain-to-negative speed effect.**
   - On gfx90a, llama.cpp quantized decode is limited by unpack cost, not bytes. Q4_K reaches 35.1% of roofline against Q8_0's 50.2% (`handoffs/active/mi210-q8-dequant-gemv-roofline.md:45`). Halving the bytes bought only +14.6% tg128 (Qwen3.6-27B: Q4_K 32.88 vs Q8 28.69 t/s, `wiki/hardware-optimization.md:2080`).
   - EXL3 decode costs more ALU per weight than Q4_K: bit-window extraction, a 32-bit integer multiply, a byte-sum and an fp16 FMA, plus Hadamard-128 on both sides (`repos/epyc-inference-research/scripts/kernel_rnd/exl3/README.md`).
   - Even tuned NVIDIA EXL3 trails 4-bit rounding formats at batch 1: c1 decode at 57–83% of AWQ-4 on H200 (intake-1528#record), and size-matched GGUF decodes 15–21% faster on RTX 5090 (intake-1509#record).
   - Production serving is DFlash2 at np4, whose verify batches (M≈9–36) go through MMQ, where bytes matter less. Batch-1 GEMV wins already failed to transfer once: a bundle measured +5.96% on the bench and −5.19% in serving (memory `project_serving_gate_first_verdict_divergence_20260908`).
4. **GGUF K-quants deliver the same VRAM saving today with no kernel work.**
   - External same-harness data at equal complete file size: GGUF Q5_K_M beats EXL3 4.0 bpw on KLD (0.0172 vs 0.0248), and EXL3 5.0 roughly ties Q6_K (0.00946 vs 0.00918). This is one vendor, on Gemma-4-12B (intake-1509#record).
   - EXL3's real advantage is quality at 2–3.5 bpw, for fitting bigger MoE models on one or two MI210s. That is strategic and months long, and there is no current target: GLM-5.3-Flash was deleted on 2026-09-22.
5. **DS41 and EXL3 barely compete for CPU, but they do compete for the MI210.**
   - DS41's planner seat is the 27B on :8083 (`handoffs/active/deepseek-v41-flash-evaluation.md:116`, DS41-C17).
   - EXL3 device runs need an exclusive MI210 with no foreign KFD processes; the launcher refuses to co-reside (`status-20260926.json` runtime_blocker).
   - Pausing DS41 does not unblock EXL3, because :8083 still serves.
   - Keep DS41 running, buy one 2–3 h :8083 drain for the cheap decisive EXL3 measurements, and run GPU-heavy EXL3 work on MI210 #2.

## 2. What works on gfx90a / ROCm 6.2

- Ours (research `9e4fa162`, `b02b1889`, `48253e78`): compiles for exact gfx90a, host-verified, 0 device runs, correctness-form only.

**Back-of-envelope (inference, unmeasured).** Batch-1 EXL3 decode looks VALU-bound on gfx90a:
- Per weight: about 5–8 VALU ops (window extract, 32-bit multiply or two 16-bit multiplies, byte-sum dot4, fp16 convert/FMA).
- MI210 throughput: about 11 T lane-ops/s.
- 27B decode: about 13–19 ms/token of ALU time, against a ~10 ms bandwidth floor at 16.5 GB.
- Result: a tuned cap near 50–75 tok/s c1 no-draft, with a realistic first tuned kernel near Q8's ~31.
- At verify widths, decode amortises if B fragments are decoded once and reused (intake-1568's M32/M64 tiles). But Q8 MMQ there is limited by grid size and compute, not bytes (`mi210-q8-dequant-gemv-roofline.md` §L3-MoE: "bf16-for-aggregate (B≥16–24), Q8 only for HBM-capacity").

## 4. DS41 vs EXL3

**DS41 so far.** After a 57.5 h, ~25-defect bring-up, the loop got 4 keeps promoted at +7.11% serving (+9.64% bench) and a runtime arm at +8.61% serving, bit-exact, on 2026-09-26/28, running on its own (memory `project_ds41_first_local_keep_20260926`; `deepseek-v41-flash-evaluation.md:1-22`).
- Risks: diminishing returns and bench-vs-serving divergence.
- Expected marginal return: a few percent per week on the CPU-served model.

**EXL3 in the same 2–4 weeks.** Roughly 0 to small for production; option value only.

**Contention:**
- **CPU:** none, unless EXL3 runs CPU sweeps or KLD jobs (keep those out of DS41 windows).
- **MI210:** yes. Draining :8083 stalls the DS41 planner (DS41-C17) and production traffic. INF-06 GPU MMQ seeds also want the GPU. Pausing DS41 does not free it.
- **Operator attention:** moderate (drains, licences, target choice).
- **Agent time:** weak.
- **MI210 #2** resolves the GPU contention. It can serve as a bench before 35B-A3B goes onto it (memory `project_speech_interlocutor_vision_20260924`).

**Verdict:** do not pause DS41.
