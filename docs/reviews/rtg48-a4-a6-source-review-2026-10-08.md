## A4 finding

The delete lens does not support “mechanize every failure.” Current source shows substantial subtraction already adopted (measurements, infra authorship, ad-hoc metrics, inline verification) and one concrete rule deletion (manual correction ledger/wrap-up memory). Typed dispatch and drain also replace named task rules with structured choke-point requirements, but retain recurring structured-input/boundary-review costs. Other candidates—fresh git-state receipt refusal, positive controls, C-ID registry, policy signature gate, and clean-only guard—are additive or deliberately unresolved; none gets a fictional “zero cost” label. The historical `03e17111` example is withdrawn as wrong-trade proof; `e08fe836` is the actual documented collision cost, while F-24 is evidence that mechanisms can themselves be defective.

A4 is answered at source level by this crosswalk. No other decision is necessary to complete A4. The separate open choices remain only (1) operator adoption of the auto-loaded-surface gate (R-8/AUD-15), and (2) operator authorization to re-land the distinct agent-typed `git clean` branch (AUD-10, implementation owner mainD); neither blocks this ruling.

## A-6 — MAIN architectural recommendation


The prompt's original binary is no longer a faithful choice: the documented system already combines structural role boundaries with mechanisms at choke points. The role file assigns deterministic tick scheduling/assignment/grants to the coordinator-daemon, judgment/decision packages/integration to coordinator-agent, and receipt relay (not measurements) to the role. BUS_PROTOCOL specifies typed dispatch fields and the daemon's selection path. R-16 is already settled by operator ruling: daemon tick may dispatch under already-granted `assign` authority, gated on H-1 and AUD-2; `fleet_watch` remains detect-only. Do not reopen or broaden that authority. Measurement policy remains human-amendment-only; agents do not change the measurement trust boundary.

Recommendation answering A6: use the structural division of authority as the primary refactor, with typed mechanisms only at existing emission/dispatch/boundary choke points. A longer prompt/mechanism list alone cannot repair mismatched ownership or make a measurement instrument trustworthy. Conversely, structural separation alone does not replace the already-ratified typed dispatch and delivery gates. Preserve daemon-loop authority exactly as R-16 settled it, preserve detect-only fleet_watch, owner-supplied measurement receipts, auditor grading, and human-only amendments to measurement policy. This is a source-architecture recommendation, not a validation that the design performs or that every gate works at runtime.


MAIN accepted this source architecture recommendation and completed A-6, preserving all settled authority and runtime gates.
