# P3-4: native MTP versus DFlash2 serving comparison — design proposal

Status: **design only; not execution-ready**. The champion promotion decision and an operator grant precede compute. P3-4 in `handoffs/completed/mi210-serving-program.md` asks about the serving duty mix and the cost of launch-bound mode changes. This proposal does not select a kernel, tenant, launch recipe, protocol amendment, threshold, or winner.

## Comparison and eligibility

Compare native MTP (A) and DFlash2 (B) on the **same full candidate serving binary**, base-model identity, hardware, host-thread placement, serving configuration, workload and sampling contract, changing only the declared speculation mode and mode-specific required draft inputs. If the base model or binary differs, the comparison confounds mode with model or implementation: stop and specify a different experiment. Before approval, pin exact binary and library digests with their source/build manifest, base and draft model identities and hashes, launch argv and environment for each arm. Demonstrate that each required model and mode is compatible with that binary. Do not infer content from a build directory, a version shared across commits, `ldd` alone, or the existence of a model file.

The existing P3-4 reference to a v9-era champion is historical. Resolve the actual candidate and its promotion decision at execution time; do not assume that candidate is the current production store. Prepare and verify inputs once before any grant. Nothing in this proposal authorizes changing frozen production, production routing, or a kernel store.

## Pre-registration before booking

Publish an immutable manifest identifying the approved source and binary/build receipts; model and draft artifacts; both full launch configurations; comparison protocol and instrument class; workload corpus, prompt and output budgets; sampling and correctness/quality oracle; target arrival patterns, concurrency and context-length distribution; warmup and observation windows; experimental unit; randomized or counterbalanced paired order and independent repetitions; hardware allocation, host topology and quiet-window/health gates; capture and retention locations; stopping rule; predeclared decision thresholds and uncertainty method. Record a checksum of the manifest before collection. Set numerical thresholds, sample size, minimum detectable effect and confidence approach from the agreed decision and pilot variance **before** the comparison, rather than choosing them after inspecting results. An operator decision is needed if an existing ratified protocol does not cover the proposed claim; do not amend the measurement constitution here.

`P-GPU-1` covers its stated GPU throughput and residency measurements but requires its production-kernel and duty-cycle conditions. Its fresh-server default is bursty; a sustained-serving decision claim needs an appropriate ratified sustained variant. `P-SERVE-SEL-1` is a selection-policy protocol and does not cover this kernel/mode throughput comparison. Experimental-candidate evidence outside an applicable ratified protocol remains observation-grade; do not imply that a later promotion retroactively changes its grade. For applicable `P-GPU-1` cells retain its required repetition count, median and MAD, acceptance counters, pre/during/post VRAM, pre/post KFD and cleanup evidence. Paired confidence intervals must follow `PAIRED-CI-1`.

The selected workloads must represent the serving duty mix, including escalation-heavy low concurrency and batch/backfill traffic, as separate strata. Define their production weights from independently supported observations; until then report each stratum separately and do not invent a weighted production aggregate. Use the same pinned workload and randomized paired order in both arms. Describe request failures, cancellations and truncated responses in the denominator and keep all samples, including outliers, under a predeclared treatment rule.

## Outcomes and mode switching

For each stratum report completed correct tasks per wall-clock time (higher is better), per-request latency distributions including TTFT and completion latency (lower is better), output tokens per second as diagnostic telemetry, correctness or quality outcomes, error rate, and uncertainty. Use a correctness oracle appropriate to the task; different draft mechanisms need not produce identical text under stochastic sampling, and text equality alone is not a quality verdict. Report quality and failures alongside speed; never hide failed requests in speed ratios. If aggregating per-item speedups, follow `MEASUREMENT.md` AGGREGATION-SPEEDUP-1: harmonic mean over correct items, beside correctness rate, without failure sentinels.

A separate mode-switch arm measures the D6 economics: drain active requests without eviction, stop the old lane through the authorized owner choreography, start and warm the other mode, and record switch start, drain completion, readiness and first successful request. Measure both directions and include downtime and any warmup cost in an explicitly defined end-to-end duty-cycle comparison. If lifecycle changes are not authorized, omit this arm and report switch economics as unmeasured; do not approximate a measured switch cost from an old launch log.

## Live evidence and verdict

For each arm, record the binary and manifest linkage proof and use the research `verify_ggml_linkage.sh` with an arm-specific `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`. Because the server dynamically loads HIP, executable `ldd` is not proof of GPU execution. Sample own-PID GPU VRAM and KFD residency **during** the request window; preserve sampling times and raw logs. Record actual process affinity, competing workload and health gates, server response records, per-request results, and teardown receipts. A sample outside the phenomenon's window proves nothing about that window. Stop or invalidate a cell on failed residency, unverified identity, unexpected co-tenant, admission/health failure, incomplete capture or failed cleanup; predeclare how partial results are classified.

Apply only the predeclared correctness, precision and materiality gates. A result may favor A or B, show a bounded no-material-difference outcome, or be inconclusive; a missing/failed gate cannot become a win. Include the metric, protocol, repetitions, date and attestation with decision-grade claims; preserve raw evidence in the required durable home before ratification. A serving result does not itself authorize kernel promotion, lane activation, registry edits or traffic changes. Wire any eventual measurement source to the belief substrate at launcher-prep time as required by the project contract.
