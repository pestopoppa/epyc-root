## Ordered implementation

1. **Resolve the exact candidate and current duty scope.** Read the champion ref/ledger, candidate ancestry and current stack registry, descriptors and compiler output. Freeze an attributable scope table with each active role, model, device, resource topology, target/drafter/projector/template, and independently evidenced best recipe for *each* arm. Record role exclusions and reasons. If the ref moved, recut M4 as one complete experimental candidate on the new tip, reconcile conflicts and redo affected prerequisite checks. Produce image manifests binding source, build settings, executable and image-local libraries; check knob presence by content. Never build or alter the frozen production tree. **Exit:** one reviewed source-to-image chain and complete scope table; no guessed recipe or silent stale pin.

2. **Resolve the decision rule before collecting.** Find the applicable, previously authorized fold policy, quality requirements and GPU per-role speed floor in the champion fold ledger and measurement constitution. Record the exact authority, metric direction, pairing, acceptance/gray/failure handling and noise treatment, plus independently evaluated resource/window/VRAM/RAM/disk margins. Preserve the historical C5/M4 C1/C2/C3 rule as a separate mechanism gate. If a floor or required decision rule does not exist, prepare the specific operator decision package; proceed with source, fixtures and descriptive-only work in parallel, and do not invent a threshold or imply promotion eligibility. Mark old 3×np grid and five CPU fresh-process pairs as historical design inputs until current scope and policy justify them.

3. **Freeze independent pre-run bindings.** Implement a reviewed canonical plan for all arms and roles, sealing the exact images and source/build manifests, role recipes and flags (baseline OFF; M4 ON explicit), model and auxiliary digests, prompt pool/seed, request and token limits, intended repeated process observations, device and placement, native input/output schemas, independently held allowlist, resource and grant boundaries, stop/deadline semantics and final result path. Record the plan digest at the original producer write boundary. Clear or reject hidden M4 environment overrides, resolve symlinks and refuse path escapes. Model hashes and actual source/image identity must be independently checked against that plan, not against a value supplied by the row itself. Revalidate the champion, scope and seals immediately before admitted work.

4. **Implement the native GPU capture and reader.** In an isolated tracked successor, adapt the actual dispatcher and native runner; do not enable the existing fail-closed prototype. Capture per-process birth identity, image-local mapped ggml digests, actual argv/environment, request start/end and raw individual completion/error records, source/plan digest, own-device KFD/VRAM samples timestamped *inside* the request interval, grant/window identity, and explicit owned-child teardown. Refuse unreadable, invalid or out-of-window samples, over-limit VRAM, wrong PID/device/library, missing cleanup and incomplete requests. Preserve partial/failure records. Recompute each aggregate from its raw requests; one fresh process is one observation. The reader independently verifies source and plan seals, rejects conflicting/missing inputs, and projects via the existing measurement class and `ClaimTuple.grade()`; no new ladder or retrospective warrants. Register only the reviewed successor and update the existing source/task when its identity is known.

5. **Complete CPU and quality paths.** Review the existing enrolled CPU producers and actual current entry against the frozen scope, full topology, image/recipe bindings and native capture contract; repair owner-loss-safe containment before enabling a successor. Carry forward failed and partial arms. Ensure required CPU quality covers the current mandated roles and both arms with original per-question responses, errors and truncation. GPU quality is supplemental; evaluate whether historical R12 applies to the newly bound exact candidate and protocol, otherwise obtain fresh paired data. Confirm strict readers and the planning-context canaries for the existing CPU and quality tasks. No source registration or fixture alone proves a measurement or a wired loop.

6. **Test the actual entry and admission.** Add deterministic model-free positive and mutation tests for source/plan/image/model/recipe/prompt tampering, hidden flags, wrong arm/PID/device/library, traversal, absent or out-of-window residency, incomplete request, changed champion, stop/hold/grant expiry and owner-loss cleanup. Exercise the *same* entry and sealed engine intended for publication with fake services, partial-result retention, normal completion, failure and actual cutoff; confirm every captured child is dead. Run repository checks and source/reader compatibility tests. Then perform the project's permitted real-entry qualification under the compute owner's current resource discipline. No test doubles become model, GPU or measurement evidence.

7. **Publish and execute by the current queue contract.** After all preflight prerequisites and exact result path/rule are bound, produce a source-sealed row with a truthful envelope, validate it through the current queue/keeper contract and send it to its owner for qualification and grant. Check for active claims and duplicates; do not edit another owner's row or start a retired launcher. Run CPU/GPU speed, necessary quality and remaining mechanism controls only within their actual grants, preserving raw output, residency in the observation window and verified cleanup. Recheck image content and source at runtime; do not infer content from path, invocation, mtime or generic version. No measurements are scheduled by this plan.

8. **Reduce and decide.** Independently replay every native record, validate per-role denominator, treatment, bindings and paired controls, and write the fold ledger verdict against the predeclared authorized rules. Incomplete/refused/failed arms remain incomplete; never pool incompatible eras or treat diagnostic load as speed. Record exact evidence and claims, project through the shared belief carrier and prove the planning-context canary. If the fold gate passes, submit the complete candidate and ledger to the champion owner for the existing fold/CAS procedure. Production deployment remains the separate versioned-kernel and operator authorization boundary.

## Completion criteria

The handoff task is complete only when the current candidate and role scope are bound, validated native CPU/GPU speed and required quality results exist for every required role, mechanism controls and the applicable predeclared acceptance rule have been evaluated, the source and planner canary are wired, and the fold ledger records a reproducible decision. A ready implementation plan is not any of those results.
