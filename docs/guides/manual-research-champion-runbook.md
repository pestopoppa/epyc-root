# Manual research into the standing champion

This procedure connects manually developed kernel work to the standing aggregate candidate and makes the resulting evidence visible. Follow [the measurement constitution](../../MEASUREMENT.md), [the measurement policy](../../agents/shared/MEASUREMENT_POLICY.md), and [the kernel manifests](../reference/kernel-manifests/README.md). They govern what a measurement or a promotion can claim. This guide does not authorize a production change or an inference run.

## Prepare the source member

1. Identify the current champion ref and full commit, current frozen production anchor and kernel-store images. Check their provenance, binary hashes and own-library linkage. A path or binary version alone is not source identity. Respect the active handoff, operator holds and inference owner's scheduling/claim rules.
2. Develop from a fresh experimental branch based on the current production tip as required by the kernel workflow; reconcile with the current champion through its fold ledger. Never build or modify a frozen production tree. Preserve the source commit, changed files, configuration, model and workload identities, recipe, artifact paths and hashes, correctness evidence and known limitations.
3. Submit the external source arm as a **member**, not as the final champion's measured gain. Review overlap and interactions with every existing member. The controller's `compatibility()` conservatively refuses overlapping changed files; when changes must coexist, resolve them as one source composition and measure it as a unit. Do not infer combined correctness or add individual effect percentages.

## Rebuild and gate the composed candidate

1. Compose a fresh full candidate from the current champion and intended member(s), preserving the existing optimizations. Bind the resulting source snapshot, parent/anchor and build recipe to the candidate evidence. Update the corresponding release-manifest row in the same commit as a fold, keep, exclusion or ownership change.
2. Build the full candidate in the experimental tree for required CPU and GPU backends. Verify artifact custody and own ggml linkage, and prove GPU residency during GPU tests. Use the exact predeclared gate recipes, decision rules, measurement units, comparison anchor and booked claims; stop if the anchor moved or the recipe's evidence cannot be bound to the actual binary. The operator-approved session and keeper rules govern who may run each gate.
3. Run correctness and performance gates on the **composed candidate itself**. In the AutoKernel controller path, `CompositionEvidence` and the matching combined candidate's T0/T1/T2 events must bind to the sealed anchor and evaluator; member performance fields do not supply combined-candidate evidence. A manual gate outcome is a separate source and must not be relabelled as a campaign T0/T1/T2 event. Record missing, failed, unsupported and partial gates as such; do not treat them as passing. Preserve all original artifacts and their hashes.
4. Only after the applicable gates pass, follow the current champion fold and release procedures. A champion ref update, candidate validation, and production promotion are different actions; satisfy each action's own authority and presence gates. Do not cherry-pick isolated features into a frozen production image.

## Attest manual gate evidence and inspect standing

The historical CH-13 carrier is `epyc.autokernel.operator_gate_bundle.v1`, with `authority: operator_gated_manual_research`, `promotion_claim: false`, and `not_campaign_sealed: true`. It is **not** `epyc.autokernel.cumulative_performance.v2`; never mint a campaign receipt from manual gates. A manual bundle is attributable observation, not promotion authority or a replacement for combined-candidate gates.

The research script `scripts/benchmark/emit_operator_gate_bundle.py` is a **specific** emitter for anchor validation, DFlash2 concurrency, and greedy-parity artifacts. It records each artifact's path and SHA-256, includes a generated-at timestamp, and marks absent gates `MISSING`. Use it only when the supplied artifacts actually match those expected schemas, measured champion and production anchor. Supply the full expected `--champion-commit`, explicit `--champion-branch`, `--production-commit`, all three explicit artifact paths, and a new dated `--out`; the emitter refuses a moved champion when the expected commit is supplied. Its built-in artifact defaults refer to historical 2026-08-28 measurements: never reuse them as evidence for a new candidate. Independently verify artifact bytes, identities and the bundle's limitations before relying on a headline.

The research `scripts/benchmark/serving_evidence_refresh.py` documents the original three-gate refresh and the dated-to-canonical publication sequence, but its hard-coded `CHAMPION_TREE`/`CHAMPION_BUILD` paths are historical. Do not run it against those paths without first reconciling the script and recipe with the current champion store, live workflow and ownership. The v10 production serving images are in `kernels/production/{cpu,gpu}`, not the frozen source tree's in-tree build directories. Any needed build or benchmark must use the applicable booking workflow; this guide does not start it.

The hub reads the canonical bundle at `/mnt/raid0/llm/autokernel/surface/operator_gate_bundle.json` through `dashboard/server.py:_read_operator_gate_bundle`; it checks schema, authority and non-promotion status and computes a freshness envelope from the measured champion's relationship to the current champion and anchor. Publish a reviewed, complete dated bundle through the authorized surface owner's procedure, then inspect the hub's `/api/loop` `operator_gates` envelope and `/loop` card for its source, anchor, measured commit, gates, missing states and freshness. A served page or green transport `/health` alone does not establish that the evidence is current; use the operator-gates freshness envelope and `/api/loop/health`. Do not silently drop missing gates or reinterpret a stale/divergent result as current.

Record the exact source and store identities, gate artifacts and hashes, the bundle path/hash, surface reading and remaining limitations in the owning handoff and progress entry. A measurement or verified finding must use the project's belief-capture and source-wiring rules. The controller contract is in `epyc-inference-research/scripts/kernel_rnd/autokernel/controller/champion.py`; the manual bundle writer is in `epyc-inference-research/scripts/benchmark/emit_operator_gate_bundle.py`. The original CH-7 and CH-13 rationale is in [the completed champion ledger](../../handoffs/completed/autokernel-champion-aggregate-completed-through-2026-09-29.md).
