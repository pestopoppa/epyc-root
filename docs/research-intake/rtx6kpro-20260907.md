## CPU-path note (prepared for INF-70 / `inf70-audit`)
(i) MoE KLD is routing-noise-prone (their 08-30 finding; formalised as the B×Q route-pinned cell in
`kld/README.md`) — INF-70's paired KLD on Flash-Next should add an expert-ID-pinned replay before any
r16-vs-uniform KLD is read as codec damage. (ii) Layer-role precision: they keep the shared expert,
layer-0 experts and the last layer high-precision for +1.7% bytes — a cheap variant for the r16 mix
(r16's production-length edge over uniform is only +0.9%). (iii) `--spec-draft-p-min` never set: fixed
full-block drafting beats early truncation for acceptance but batch-wide suppression hurts tokens/step;
INF-70 already lists the knob as untested — test it with steps/s, not tok/s. (iv) Their MTP-at-concurrency
data (flat +50% to C32) is consistent with INF-70's C=4 1.13×. Host sysctls `kernel.numa_balancing=0`,
`sched_migration_cost_ns=50000` are unverified on our EPYC (2-minute check). Not transferable to CPU:
all their kernels, KV offload (model resident), DCP.

## Contradictions with our own record (flagged, not resolved)
1. Spec-dec gain vs concurrency: rule says single-stream only; external MTP flat +51% C1→+52% C32 and our
   DF2-5 +28%→+48% 1→8 in-flight both contradict → RULE-RESCOPE filed.
2. DFlash2 at sustained C8 on Qwen3.8-27B: external Discord "+69% C1 / −19% C8" vs our +47.8% at 8 in-flight
   (different quant/engine/K) → SL-4 adds the sustained-C8 cell.
3. DFlash2 vs MTP not a portable prior: external qualified table has DFlash2-7 SLOWER than MTP3 on GLM-5.3
   (their own digest claimed ≈2×); consistent with "each model needs its own drafter" (Qwopus 0.356).
4. Losslessness at temp>0: external "DFlash only valid at temp=0"; our DF2-6 7/12 for BOTH DFlash2 and
   draft_simple at identical indices → DF2-RNG lead.
5. KV-quant speed: their FP8 KV is speed-neutral hardware cast; ours is a block dequant (−6.9%/−16.7% at
   64k); TurboQuant-in-llama.cpp "8× slower" is a third point against KV quant here. Conclusion stands.
6. KLD on MoE may be dominated by routing flips → R23-47 route-pinned cell.
7. Their "MTP does not degrade quality" claim is contradicted by their own noise-floor control; do not cite.
8. HIP-graph "4.3×" is vs an eager baseline — ours is +4..14% base; do not import 4× as an expectation.
9. Power: they scale +30% at C64 from 300→600 W; our MI210 draws ~200 W of 300 W — but re-check draw DURING
   np4 serving (only proven under llama-bench/GEMM windows).
10. Bench≠serving: their 8 documented microbench-vs-E2E inversions independently confirm R23-43; seed 19
    is the first mechanistic cause on our side (MMVQ optimised, MMQ served).
