## Admission and booking

Before booking, finish CS-5 (both candidate harnesses and pinned model/weight/runner/runtime revisions), and verify the completed CS-3 write side is emitted by each harness, seal CS-4 corpus with disjoint selection and confirmation manifests, including real en/it paralinguistic recordings, and stage the cascade on identical clips. Pin and verify all source, corpus and model inputs *before* grants. Confirm P-VOICE protocol/thresholds are ratified by the operator through CS-2 before treating a voice-turn claim as an Annex S result. Until then collect explicitly labelled exploratory observations; do not claim a ratified release gate. Freeze per-arm prompts, decoding, audio sample rate, timeouts, repetitions, scorer, failure denominator, selection/confirmation policy and gate thresholds in a run-plan commit before the first scored run. In particular the current G1 requirement is en and it intelligible; numerical G1/G2/G3 thresholds and human rating rubric are not supplied, and must not be invented after seeing outputs. Include every exposed clip, timeout, crash, corrupted/empty output and never-fire in the denominator. Use selection to choose settings and touch held-out confirmation once. Prove the runner emits `protocol_id`, reps, attestation and `metric_direction` on write; retain raw per-item inputs/outputs, output audio, timing events, configs, result packet and failures.

Time estimates below are **provisional booking allowances**, not measured ETAs: assume a ready harness, already resident local assets and pre-verified corpus, a 30–60 min exclusive timing cell, and human listen/scoring asynchronously outside GPU occupancy. Runtime depends on corpus size, model load and repetitions; calculate final estimates from a short non-scored pilot *before* booking and amend the predeclared plan before scored work. Ranges include load/unload and verification but not retries or operator review. If an arm overruns, preserve partial outputs and stop at the grant boundary; schedule a successor without silently redrawing exposed items. Book multiple windows, not an invented single deadline.
