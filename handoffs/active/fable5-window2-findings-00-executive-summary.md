## Verdicts on the brief's five hypotheses (§6)

1. *"Integrity is largely solved; only W5 remains, and that is an objective-specification question."* — **Half-confirmed, and the half matters.** Promotion integrity: solved by construction (verified gate chain, findings-01 §1). Learning-surface integrity: **not solved** — three named gaps (refutation loop, fingerprint multiplicity, strategy-store writes), each with a cheap by-construction close. W5 is *not* an objective-specification question — it is a stale-era measurement plus a protocol that post-determinism cannot measure what it claims to (findings-01 §3).
2. *"W7 gives the guarantee by construction."* — **Refuted.** W7 is 2½ structural items (eval-sampling clamp, answer-text non-exposure, session hygiene) plus detection and incentive hygiene. The anti-gaming centerpiece (W6) is a one-sided, after-the-fact alarm that gates authority labels, not the running loop (findings-01 §2).
3. *"core_v2's no-go is the instrument correctly rejecting a mis-specified objective."* — **Refuted as stated.** The no-go was right *operationally* (refusing to enshrine a core selected for flakiness) but the 33/40 is manufactured: infra errors + decommissioned sampling era + estimator resolution below the pass gate. Neither "objective wrong" nor "instrument broken": **measurement era invalid, protocol now structurally obsolete** (findings-01 §3).
4. *"We keep optimizing within unquestioned framings — which do we drop first?"* — **Drop "the GPU program is a model-offload problem."** It is a fleet-placement and instrument-throughput problem (findings-02 §1). Second: "clean-window scarcity is a fixed cost" — it is a consequence of single-substrate serving. Third: "same-seed repeatability = item difficulty" (findings-01 §3).
5. *"The window-1 reversal holds: frontdoor residency headline, GPU-drafting weakest leg; α gates the GPU-draft leg."* — **Confirmed and strengthened.** Residency now has hardware-measured support (47% roofline at Q8; GDN runs clean on HIP) and the eval-engine coincides with it (frontdoor is the eval bottleneck role). The draft leg is further weakened: every role already runs MTP self-spec in production, so the drafter must beat a live baseline whose acceptance stats are already in your logs (R10). One refinement: α is measurable at zero cost *first* (log read), so the "α forks the program" framing softens — residency does not wait on α at all (findings-02 §3–4).
