## Problem
The lean orchestrator registry (`epyc-orchestrator/orchestration/model_registry.yaml`) is now compiled from the master research registry (`epyc-inference-research/orchestration/model_registry.yaml`) by `src/registry/registry_compiler.py` at normal stack launch. Before 2026-06-27, `--compile-registry` was default-off because master and lean disagreed on active launch facts. That drift class is now closed: checked-in lean equals the compiler output, and `orchestrator_stack.py start` compiles by default.

The current remaining work is not master/lean output-shape reconciliation or worker recipe migration. It is follow-on cleanup: keep topology-only constants in launcher code, close two `vision_escalation` strict evidence gaps if strict mode is required, and eventually remove obsolete hand-edit annotations.

## Objective
Keep the master registry as the single source of truth for generated lean runtime facts, and finish the remaining launcher/strict-evidence cleanup around that default-on compile path.
