## Dashboard CPU-region lock panel hardening

the dashboard CPU-region lock panel against the embedder monitoring question.

servers and unloaded/running activity buckets, while `/dashboard/api/region_locks`
derives rows only from roles with non-empty q0..q3 CPU-region topology. That is
the intended split: embedders are stack services to monitor, but they do not
belong in the CPU-region lock grid when they have no physical lock footprint.
