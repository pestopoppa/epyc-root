## X-MAS cheap-first regression repair

Landed `epyc-orchestrator` `f517902d` (`Preserve cheap-first under X-MAS
cheap-role enforce`) after diagnosing the held constrained-policy A/B
(`benchmarks/results/runs/xmas_live_ab/20260621T112005Z-constrained-policy`).
The regression was not a simple wrong-role table issue: the negative same-cell
rows often had the same final `worker_general` route in baseline and X-MAS, but
X-MAS had marked the route as applied and the live chat handler then skipped
the try-cheap-first path. Baseline kept the successful cheap-first response;
X-MAS forced the normal handler path for the same cheap role and lost quality.

override selects a role different from the configured cheap-first role. If
X-MAS enforces the cheap role itself, cheap-first remains eligible. The
response schema now also exposes optional `xmas_meta`, and
`scripts/benchmark/xmas_live_ab.py` records it in result rows so the next A/B
can prove apply reason, incumbent role, and suggested role instead of inferring
from `routing_strategy` strings.
