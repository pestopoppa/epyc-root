### OBS-6, P17.BT-5a retrospective feedback synthesizer (RPL-AP)

**Source.** Both AutoPilot journal shards, read-only, analysed from a hash-verified scratch copy. Originals were
re-verified unchanged after the run:

- `autopilot_journal.jsonl`: SHA-256 `1c5a665922de5c81c53cc6dd4218a9597123c1a1d42347344104683758f70dbc`
- `autopilot_journal_1.jsonl`: SHA-256 `9f9fd39b8ed1db90b073782b9fe4c57a368b6c6e9c9bef6d4819b4b98aec408a`

That is 1,372 trial entries with 10 supersession events folded.

**Code built.** Orchestrator `95d663e5`: `scripts/autopilot/feedback_synthesizer.py` and
`tests/unit/test_feedback_synthesizer.py` (7 tests; 11/11 with the AP-ME-1 tests). The tests cover the trust filter,
hint sign, no-future-leak, a synthetic SIGNAL corpus, noise and small-corpus refusals, and all-shard read-only.

**Method.**

- **Pairs.** Pairs are learning-eligible numeric trials with numeric params. Each trial's score is its quality minus
  the median of the 10 preceding same-tier eligible trials.
- **Hints.** Hints are fitted on strictly earlier trials only. A parameter gets a hint when |Spearman rho| ≥ 0.30.
  The hint is directional when its bootstrap sign stability is ≥ 0.80 and non-directional otherwise.
- **Baselines.** The no-feedback baseline is the historical success rate of hinted trials, where success means a
  positive score. The control scrambles the hint signs (2,000 reps).

**Stop rule (predeclared).** There is one look at the full fixed corpus, with no interim looks and no threshold
changes. The verdict is INCONCLUSIVE when there are fewer than 20 hinted trials or fewer than 8 in either arm. It is
SIGNAL when uplift is ≥ 0.10 and p ≤ 0.05. Otherwise it is NO_SIGNAL.

**Result: INCONCLUSIVE_INSUFFICIENT_COVERAGE.** There are 87 pairs (trials 1058–1505), but only 8 trials were hinted
on a parameter they touched.

| Arm | Trials | Success rate |
|---|---|---|
| Follow | 6 | 0.17 |
| Oppose | 2 | 1.00 |
| No-feedback baseline | 8 | 0.375 |

The uplift is −0.21 and the scrambled-control p is 0.75. The secondary speed objective is also inconclusive (9
hinted). The threshold-free coverage ceiling is 21 hinted trials. 216 numeric_trial rows logged empty params,
including every shard-0 row. They are not pairs and were not back-filled.

**Record.** `t3-evidence/ap-p17bt5a-backtest-20261102.json`, SHA-256
`b229ea0680f96632f413f6544ec33bb56b875f9b52560a440eb5726e0f687612`. The record is deterministic: a re-run gave an
identical hash.

**Consumer.** P17.BT-5a gets a dated sub-bullet with no tick, because an inconclusive bench does not answer the
