## Lessons
1. **Comparisons across builds or days are invalid.** This happened twice today: the NPD1-STEP4 TTFT claim and the C5-vs-C6 read. Compare only within one run, with ABBA.
2. **Idle-holding rows** waste windows. A row that holds a grant while idle must be stopped or demoted.
3. **Always use the most advanced champion (C6)** for new diagnostics; C5/lbt results needed re-runs.
4. **Failure alerts went unread until the inference mailbox existed** (`b35d36092`, `50d65faf4`).
5. **Deleting a model broke a row**. Before a reclaim deletes a model, check which queued rows reference it.
6. **A tool that rewrites its input in place** (audit.py / llvm-objcopy) silently corrupted store binaries. Audits must work on copies and verify SHA256SUMS afterwards.
