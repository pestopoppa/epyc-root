### np × context throughput finding (bandwidth-bound) — feeds TB-6
Batching does NOT transfer across context. 122B-IQ2 at 36864-ctx: np=14 collapsed (per-req decode ~2 t/s →
timeouts); np=4 gave ~47 agg (~8% over single-stream); the "18.2 agg" first reported was a corrupted metric
(real tokens ÷ timeout-inflated wall). **KV read/step scales with context×slots + MoE expert-scatter →
aggregate DROPS as concurrency rises at large context; optimal np → 1 as reasoning budget grows.** The
pre-promotion "148.7 agg@B32" was small-context (no contradiction). Binding constraint = memory BANDWIDTH,
not VRAM capacity. Committed root `1aea6471`.

### IQ2 termination defect (the real architect caveat)
122B-**IQ2** loops on `\boxed{answer}` to the token cap on **25%** of items (Q8 arms 0–1%) — degenerate
repetition / EOS damage tracking **quantization aggressiveness**, not size. Post-extractor-fix it costs **no
accuracy** but **~2× tokens** (median 6195 vs 4019) — a production *operating* cost. Production gives the
architect NO repetition penalty (bench matched that → loop occurs live). **Operator policy: apply repetition
penalty SELECTIVELY to high-quant models, never blanket** (it has a quality cost). Filed RP-1..4 in
reasoning-effort-levels.md (`e885b4a6`). **RP-1 probe running at wrap** (repeat 1.1/1.3/DRY on the 40 looping
items) — results to be logged next window (operator-approved deferral).
