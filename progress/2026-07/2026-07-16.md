## Frontier replay quality caveat correction

Follow-up audit found that the frontier numeric replay was an exact parameter replay, not an exact historical question-vector replay. The replay qids exactly match the current EvalTower `legacy_pool_seed_42_n50` helper sample, but overlap the historical frontier rows by only `8/50` qids for trial `996` and `7/50` qids for trial `1003`.

The raw replay quality drop is therefore contaminated by vector drift and by runtime errors. Historical rows had `34/50` and `35/50` correct with one error each; replay rows had `29/50` and `30/50` correct with seven errors each. The six additional errors are enough to explain the five-correct quality drop, and two hard replay errors per run came from the now-fixed plan-review reroute-target bug (`bc8d3303`).

Durable report corrected: `/mnt/raid0/llm/epyc-orchestrator/orchestration/reports/frontier_numeric_replay_20260716.md`. The speed regression remains valid as a current EvalTower/vector floor probe; the replay quality numbers should not be promoted as clean post-fix model-quality evidence without a paired qid rerun.
