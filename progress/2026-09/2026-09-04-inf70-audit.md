2026-09-01 19:40, two days before `99425578d` fixed the IQ4_XS `Q8_K_R16` repack at `Ny>=32`;

earlier returned an inert null.

**Method note worth keeping.** The earlier cross-binary figure (4.4798 fresh vs 4.9043 stale) was
refused as evidence at the time, correctly: two binaries confound the kernel flag with every other
build difference. Only the one-binary form isolates the variable. The refusal, not the number, is
the reusable part.

the production kernel configuration rather than one nobody serves. Culprit tensor

in serving — so the bug disabled the sole gate that would have detected it, and "never seen in
production" was not evidence of its absence.
