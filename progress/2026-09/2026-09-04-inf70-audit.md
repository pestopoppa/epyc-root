**The correction**: I wrote that the kernel paths "agree within overlapping error bars." The bars do overlap,
but that reading is wrong, because the exact-digit reproduction proves the runs are DETERMINISTIC. The ± is
corpus-sampling uncertainty, not run-to-run noise, so the 2.2% gap between `IQK=1` and `IQK=0` is a real
systematic offset (the lossy Q8 repack at large Ny, by design), not noise the bars absorb. Operationally:
**every PPL number on this model must name its `GGML_IQK` state or it is not comparable.**
