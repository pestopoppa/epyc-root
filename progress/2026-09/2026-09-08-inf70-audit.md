### ⚠⚠ The 0.80% floor is valid ONLY while the GPU loop is down — never quote it bare

Raised by the autokernel session 2026-09-08, and it qualifies a number this session had been quoting
without its conditions — **the exact defect we spent 2026-09-07 filing against other people's
numbers**. The floor was measured **during the run-30 drain**: run 30 was draining or stopped
throughout HARNESS-1's hot session, so 0.80% is the **quiet-host** figure, not the general one.

With the loop running, `jobs=64` builds occupy **96-183** — the SMT siblings of **88 of our 96 bench
cores** — plus GPU host threads on **184-191** covering the other 8. **The applicable floor under the
loop is UNMEASURED and is certainly worse.** Per U2's own rule, a floor carries its **harness, n,
contention model AND host state**; a bare number is not a floor.

### ⚠ CRITICAL CAVEAT — never compare a hot absolute against a cold one

**Hot reads +4.36% above cold on the same binary with byte-identical output.** Prefill moved only
+1.8%, which a **memory-state** explanation predicts and a **drift** explanation does not. Hot and
cold absolutes are two different scales; only within-scale deltas are comparable.
