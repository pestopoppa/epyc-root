Dispatch-side facts that had no tasks anywhere: every build in the fusion tree is OpenMP (gcc+libgomp,
flat `omp barrier`; `--poll` is a no-op there); one full-team barrier after every non-empty node with no
elision; a second internal barrier inside every batch-1 `mul_mat` (the single activation row's
quantization split 48 ways) and `mul_mat_id` (serial grouping by thread 0 + a 512-entry scan); 14 rows per
thread for a 640-row projection with no small-matrix threshold; only `RMS_NORM+MUL` fused on CPU; THP
`enabled=[always]` on the host but ggml never requests huge pages (`posix_memalign(64)`), and the
2026-05-28 record shows khugepaged cannot coalesce a bench-window buffer; `tests/test-barrier.cpp`
exists as a harness (internal threadpool only).

MTP facts: our `conversion/qwen4exp.py` drops the head by policy (`supports_mtp_export = False`);
the base converter exports `nextn.*` heads for six other archs; production v9 already ships the
`--spec-type draft-mtp` driver needing only a `t_h_nextn` export in `qwen4exp.cpp` and an `mtp-*.gguf`.
No HF source remains on disk (the 08-28 `rm -rf`), so it strictly needs the `Qwen/Qwen3.8-Flash-Next-FP8`
re-download (~185 GB); the OP-32 Option-C disk blocker is gone (736 GB free).
