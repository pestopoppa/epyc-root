## Root causes

- **The 220 GB/s premise measured the wrong shape.** `bench_readbw` at 2026-09-26T10:55Z (raw:
  `/mnt/raid0/llm/tmp/ds41-scope-20260926/readbw-20260926T105535Z.txt`, script `readbw_gap.sh` alongside):

  | screen | cores | threads | read-sum GB/s | gemv-2560 GB/s |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | full | 0-95 | 48 | **399.6** | 377.5 |
  | full | 0-95 | 96 | **449.4** | 475.9 |
  | half | 0-47 | 24 | 212.4 | 218.1 |
  | half | 0-47 | 48 | 275.3 | 281.5 |

  - The "220 GB/s ceiling" is roughly the **half screen at 24 threads**. It is the wrong number for the full screen.
  - Recording conditions: `interleave=all`, OMP stack spread/cores/active, reps=5, 2 GiB arrays, no hugepages.
    The load average at start was 17-21, so this is **not an idle-host reading**.
  - The INF-70 C0 figures (152.6 / 165.6 GB/s read-sum, 2026-09-02) predate the 2026-09-21 BIOS change, and DS41-C6's
    "~165 GB/s pattern ceiling" row is stale for the same reason.
- **Serving-shape decomposition** (scope agent, from the in-loop node profile):
  - dense Q8_0 tinyBLAS: 40% of the cycle at ~52% of peak;
  - `MUL_MAT_ID` Q4_K: 32% at 82-94%;
  - barrier/straggler wall: ~21%.

  The headroom is in the dense Q8_0 path and in the barrier wall, not in the expert gather the planner kept
  targeting. The inbox note `store/inbox/40-ds41-scope-widening-20260926.md` (with addendum) now drives the planner
  with these numbers.
- **opencode silently capped `max_tokens` at 32000** unless `OPENCODE_EXPERIMENTAL_OUTPUT_TOKEN_MAX` is set, so
  larger requested author/planner output budgets never took effect.
- **Resume was keyed on the full epoch.** The full epoch folds the actor roster in through
  `enrolled_manifest_digest`. The critic switch moved it from `e0aefe6a` to `e384c2ad`, which orphaned every resume
  checkpoint even though anchor, target, recipe, instrument and floor were unchanged.
