## Operator question: "are we using the model's n-gram corpus effectively?" — analysed, B7 filed, P0 lead found

Zero-inference analysis from source + existing measurements (no bench lock).
- **Structurally yes.** `per_layer_token_embd` (51B params, IQ4_NL, ~27 GB) is read via `ggml_get_rows`
  (`qwen4exp.cpp:1115`), not a GEMM. It adds ~0 to the 4.16 GB/token stream and costs DRAM *latency* only — 27 GB
  resident at zero bandwidth is exactly the CPU+RAM thesis for choosing this model, and no GPU here could hold it.
- **Its one real cost was already fixed.** The PLE gather WAS the single-threaded `GET_ROWS` at 9.34 ms/token
  (~10% of the token); D8 parallelised it to 2.59 ms (~3%) — the largest single gain of 2026-09-02, merged in the tip.
- **Verified correct**, incidentally, by the P0 node trace: the PLE conv is bit-identical at n=3, and despite IQ4_NL
  being on the repack list the PLE never takes that path (gather, not matmul).
- **Filed B7** for the two unmeasured items: (a) a PLE ablation — nobody has priced its 27 GB + 2.6 ms/token against
  its quality contribution; (b) precision UP — the PLE is the cheapest family on this box to spend precision on since
  it costs ~no bandwidth (B4 optimised the opposite direction), but not free: IQ4_NL→Q8_0 doubles table and gather.
- **Recorded as do-not-pursue**: the PLE as a free drafter. It stores per-layer *embeddings* keyed by n-gram hash,
  not next-token distributions; `ngram-mod` is a context self-copy drafter and unrelated (hence its retracted 2.8×).
