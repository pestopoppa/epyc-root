## Implemented (research `sub/e1a-niah-20260916` @ `d8fab068`, pending merge, not pushed)
- `scripts/benchmark/niah_scorer.py` scores every response twice:
  - **strict** is a raw exact match that forgives only outer whitespace.
  - **lenient** normalizes both sides (NFKC, format characters stripped, casefolded, whitespace
    collapsed) and then needs the answer as a whole-word substring of the response.
- An empty reference is undecidable and is left out of both denominators.
- The summary reports `strict_accuracy` and `lenient_accuracy` together (higher is better), plus
  `format_gap` and `format_only_failures`, which are diagnostic and have no direction.
- The scorer uses the standard library only and never calls an LLM judge. Its CLI replays saved
  JSONL with configurable field names and writes byte-identical output for identical input.
- `scripts/benchmark/test_niah_scorer.py` has 29 tests on synthetic haystacks and passes. Among
  them: a property test that a strict pass always implies a lenient pass, and a test that
  `RULERAdapter` NIAH prompts score without a converter. `test_answer_scoring.py` still passes
  (18 tests).
