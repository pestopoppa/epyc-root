(MUL_MAT/MUL_MAT_ID op-test), ran 25 h from 2026-09-29 01:05Z at nice 19 on ~4-6 cores (6 d CPU time) with its

  at call end by pidfd TERM→KILL with verified dead; ak-check PDEATHSIG, group kill, signal handlers and
  inherited fence fds; a MeasurementWatch that invalidates a CPU launch on campaign-process contention (two
