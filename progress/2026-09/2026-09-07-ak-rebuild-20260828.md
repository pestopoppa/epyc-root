  reclaimed; `VACUUM` 133 s; `PRAGMA quick_check` = ok; `-wal`/`-shm` gone; no holders.
- **Root cause of the slowness: Debian/Ubuntu libsqlite3 (3.46.1) is compiled with `SQLITE_SECURE_DELETE`**,
  so every freed page is zero-filled and written through the WAL — deleting ~200 GB of rows WROTE ~270 GB
  (measured ~4.5 MB/s with run 30 sharing the RAID). `PRAGMA secure_delete=OFF` on the deleting connection
  is the fix; the pruner now sets it. Second lesson: two concurrent instances of the same script race for
  the write lock (one dies with `database is locked` after the 60 s busy timeout) and a Ctrl-Z'd instance
  holds its transaction open — run mass deletes ONCE, in ONE terminal, foreground.
