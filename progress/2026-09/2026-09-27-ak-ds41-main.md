  - Findings: `/mnt/raid0/llm/tmp/ds41-c57-cpu0-20260927/DS41-C57-findings-and-decision-packages.md`.
  - The ~10 µs of dead time per hc node is sleeping libgomp barriers: 60-95k voluntary switches per OMP thread.
  - The sleep is triggered by the fast loader's AUTO reader team (`llama-model-loader.cpp:1689`, `min(32, max)`).
    It runs 32 threads before the 48-thread compute team under `OMP_PLACES=cores` (96 places), which leaves libgomp
    throttled for the life of the process.
  - `--load-threads 48`, or a 48-place list, spins instead.

  | Regime | Arms | tok/s | Request spread |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | spin | A2 `{2}:47:2,{1}`, L48 `--load-threads 48` | 22.4-22.6 | ≤0.3% |
  | sleep | `cores`, `GGML_REPACK_THREADS=48`, `OMP_NUM_THREADS=48` | 19.6-20.6 | up to 13% |

  - That is about **+11%** for spinning. CPU 0 itself costs ≤0.2% (run-queue wait).
  - Production resolves LLVM libomp with `KMP_BLOCKTIME=10`, so production is expected to be unaffected. That is
    unmeasured.
