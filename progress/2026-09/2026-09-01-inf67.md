## The operator's corrections (all accepted and recorded)

1. The blocker is the 1141 ms gemv (3.3x the graph's whole 1T decode), not the 215 ms
   churn.
2. The cause: the per-row unbatched vec_dot in `FusedMM::dot`/`lora_mm` — the exact
   anti-pattern from the 2026-08-28 HC_MIX record (785 vs 150 us — the mul_mat kernel
   batches rows; unbatched quantized dots lose).
3. The extrapolation was optimistic: the graph gains only ~4.7x from 1T to 48T (bandwidth-
   bound); at ~5x the fused lands at ~445 ms/token (~2.2 t/s) vs the graph's 13.5 — ~6x.
4. The 84%-gemv split is non-discriminating (a 1-thread graph would also show ~85-95%
   gemv); the control arm (graph-1T 350 ms vs fused-1T 1350 ms) says the megakernel does
   not currently deliver.

## The go/no-go (answered)

YES, structurally: the batched mul_mat kernel is callable on staged tensors (the pattern
the fused already uses for the GDN/flash/silu kernels). The next step: the staged batched
`lora_mm` with the kernel's wdata/threadpool/strides handled — the day's work. If it
delivers the ~5x (the HC_MIX precedent), the design is viable and the bit-exactness hunt
resumes; if the fusion structurally requires per-row dots, the design is refuted on its own
terms.
