# Vidya SC3–SC11 — measurements enter the belief kernel, and the contract that lets the next source in

**Problem.** The belief substrate modelled only what we *read*. Across 4,224 beliefs the Q axis held
**zero** claims at `Witnessed`, because the only adapter read `intake_index.yaml` and an intake entry
is a literature record. A quarter of the warrant carrier was unreachable for a reason that was purely
about which door the data came through.

**Root cause, and a wrong diagnosis corrected.** The prior session priced a progress-log adapter and
concluded *"our own measurements are recorded as prose too"* — 105 of 4,687 stated results (2.2%)
citing anything durable. **That conclusion measured the NARRATION layer and generalised it to the
measurement layer**, and the operator challenged it directly: autopilot, autokernel and the
kernel-freeze procedure follow an explicit constitution. They do, and none of them writes to progress
markdown, so the statistic said nothing about them. Verified structured corpus: **47 tracked
ratification JSONs (34 hashed), 4,562 measurement-shaped tracked files in the research repo, 14
`artifacts/**/manifest.json` of which 6 are SEALED.** `Q4 Witnessed` was never unreachable in
principle — nothing had read the directory. The narrow finding (prose narration is unattested)
survives; the generalisation is withdrawn, recorded in the deep dive rather than edited away.

## Bugs found and fixed — four of them self-inflicted, which is the point

1. **Sealed-manifest identity collision.** v1 keyed claims on the manifest directory *basename*. On
   the real tree `sealed_package` names two runs and `input` names three **arms of one A/B**, so 6
   manifests folded into 3 claims and three arms merged into a single belief. The fake-identity
   failure this substrate exists to detect, produced by its own adapter. Caught only by checking
   output count against input count. Fixed to a path-relative identity; 12 frames retracted.
2. **A coverage gap reported as decay.** SC5 flagged nine entries as "lost all support" when none had
   ever had a claim ingested — a gap in us, not rot in the page. Split into `uningested` vs
   `unsupported`; headline moved 16 → 12 stale pages, and real decay is **zero**.
3. **The reps extractor understated by 40 points.** It read only modern denominator keys and found
   `reps` on 46% of trial rows. Probing the gap found 545 older rows carrying it under
   `details.total`. Recovery took coverage to **86%** — and since `total` counts what was ATTEMPTED
   while `quality_denominator` counts what SCORED, the tuple now records `reps_basis`.
4. **Two graders for one rule.** `measurement_record.grade()` and `sealed_manifest.grade()` both
   implemented the constitution and disagreed: `Judged/T0` vs `Judged/Located`. Resolved by making
   one ladder and delegating; the conformance test now fails any adapter that grades privately.

Two further defects were caught by *existing* tests while building the framework: requiring `date`
structurally made dateless runs unrepresentable, and deriving artifact presence from the tuple path
silently downgraded every sealed run to `Anchored`.

## Autokernel — the question that inverted the plan

Asked whether autokernel needed the same hook. **It did not: it is stricter.** Its schema enforces
`claim_grammar` as a REQUIRED block (category, protocol_id, metric, metric_direction, reps ≥ 1,
attestation_ref) plus a required `anchor`, hex-sha256 `run_id`, and INVALID runs journaled rather
than discarded. Its own docstring names AutoPilot as *"the predecessor loop [that] had no such
contract"*. So autopilot was aligned to autokernel's vocabulary instead — which surfaced a trap: the
optimiser declares `directions=["maximize"]*4` over `(quality, speed, -cost, reliability)`, so the
raw `cost` field is **lower_better**. Reading the maximize-list alone inverts it. Direction is now
recorded, never inferred. AutoKernel has emitted **zero** `evaluation_event` records, so no adapter
is warranted yet (SC10).

## Rejected on evidence

The obvious next step — ingest the other 4,562 measurement files — was **priced and rejected**.
Sampling 50 files from each of the four largest areas (200 total): **zero carry the full tuple**;
`reps` is essentially never recorded and `sha256` is absent from three of four areas. An adapter
would add ~4,500 claims all capped at `Verified/Located`, gating nothing — the identical trap SC2
identified for prose. Also filed SC6-HAZARD: support is counted by *source locator*, so 2,605 result
files measuring one thing would read as 2,605 independent witnesses.

## Date-stamp correction (found during this wrap-up)

Work completed today was stamped **2026-08-12** throughout — handoff checkboxes, the spec section,
code comments, the README, the memory entry and the explainer. The real date is **2026-08-10**. In a
program whose subject is provenance that is not cosmetic, so 37 stamps were corrected across 15 files
in both repos. Synthetic fixture dates in tests were deliberately left alone (arbitrary literals, not
claims). The ledger frames appended today carry `created_at` two days ahead; verified **cosmetic, not
functional** — folding at the true date returns identical results (4,233 beliefs, Witnessed 6),
because the fold does not exclude on `created_at`.

**The audit that opened this.** Operator asked what consumes the beliefs. Answer, measured: a grep
across `scripts/`, `repos/epyc-orchestrator/scripts/` and `.claude/` found **zero references to the
fold outside `scripts/vidya/`**, and the only projection artifact on disk was a 2026-08-09 demo
(477 lines of `clm_intake_960_04 is supported at Verified/Located` — machine-readable, human-useless).
The engine was complete and wired to nothing.

### The gate found real damage on its first run

Not synthetic. `handoffs/active/intake-derived-work-2026-07-25.md` — a handoff whose whole subject is
work *derived from* intake entries — rests on **intake-896**, whose claim 03 a Stage-2 dive found
**fabricated by a Stage-1 summariser** (the source post contains two generic sentences about
`/doctor` and none of the four behaviours attributed to it). `wiki/knowledge-management.md` and
`docs/design/vidya-pilot-corpus.md` cite it too. Separately, three documents rest on **intake-110**
claim 04 — a "57–59% token reduction while improving accuracy by 9–16 points" figure whose `+9–16`
half the authors revised away upstream, per their own Appendix D.

### Four defects found while building, three of them pre-existing

1. **The run-form scanner invented an entry.** `citation_gate` reported a dangling `intake-2602`.
   Source text: `(intake-374/378/2602.11149 synthesis)` — a two-entry run followed by an arXiv id,
   and SC5's pattern ate `/2602` out of `2602.11149`. The first fix let the regex backtrack into the
   partial number `260` and invent a *different* bogus entry; a `\b` anchor closes it. SC5's own
   numbers are unchanged (707 edges, 28 pages) — the bad citation was in a handoff, not a wiki page.
2. **485 correction frames carry 155 distinct corrections.** `per_claim_effects` was added to the
   correction assertion as `{...} or None`, so every correction on an entry without per-claim
   verdicts started carrying an explicit null, which changed the dedup key and re-emitted the corpus.
   `fold` blocks a claim while ANY copy is unreviewed, so reviewing three of four copies leaves it
   blocked with nothing to show why. The queue now groups by content and emits one `correction_reviewed`
   per copy. **Honest scope**: `_dedup_key` now drops nulls, but a re-ingest today was *already*
   idempotent (0 of 9,661 frames would be emitted) because the ledger holds the widened variant — the
   change prevents the next additive field from repeating it, it does not fix a live bug.
3. **The queue missed a claim that only matches after alias resolution.** Reading claim ids from the
   correction's own assertion accounted for 570 of 571 blocked claims; `clm_intake_374_03` is reached
   only through the alias map. Claim ids are now read back **out of the fold** rather than recomputed
   — the same lesson as the two-graders defect earlier today.
4. **A gate that fires on everything gets switched off.** At floor `Verified/Located` the citation
   gate flags 1,520 of 1,754 citations `weak` and buries the 9 actionable ones; at `Hinted/Located`
   it flags 5. Default set from the measurement, not from taste, and `review` warns rather than
   blocks for the same reason spec §10 caps obligation surfacing.

### Autopilot's planner has independently reinvented this kernel

Operator asked whether the kernel would help the planners or bottleneck them. `operator_hypotheses.py`
already carries a **mandatory falsifier** ("refused at load"), an append-only resolution ledger where
"a refutation is exactly as recordable as a confirmation", and `evidence_trial_ids: []` **refused** on
a prior because "being the operator's idea is not new evidence". Append-only, provenance-graded,
refutation-first, authorship≠authority — convergent evolution on four of the kernel's answers.

Three gaps it cannot close alone: no retraction propagation when a resolving trial is later
invalidated; three states instead of a lattice, so "one noisy run" and "sealed measurement" are
indistinguishable; and loop-local storage AutoKernel cannot read.

**Cost is not the obstacle** — fold is 0.08 s over 20,328 frames, a gate query 0.001 ms. The bottleneck
risk is semantic: gating hypothesis *generation* would turn a discovery loop into a justification
loop. Wire at **promotion** instead (trials are already born `CANDIDATE` from SC6). Filed as SC14,
blocked on the session that owns `autopilot.py`.

## SC12-FIX … SC17 — the consumers meet the corpus, and three of six findings were the tool's fault

**Operator asked to fix the flagged citations.** Doing so turned the citation gate on its own first
report, and the result is the more useful half of the session.

### The false-positive rate was 50%, and finding that out was worth more than the fixes

Of six "overturned" citations, **three were documents that RECORDED the fabrication they were flagged
for**. `intake-derived-work-2026-07-25.md` literally says the `/doctor` description "was invented and
has been struck". `wiki/knowledge-management.md` uses it as a worked example of a correction that
never landed. `docs/design/vidya-pilot-corpus.md` lists it as corpus. None rests on the claim.

The gate could not distinguish **relying on** a claim from **discussing the record**. Added
`intake-NNN#record`: non-blocking, still reports `dangling` (an entry that does not exist cannot be
discussed either), and deliberately **still counted as an SC5 dependency edge** — "which pages name
this entry" and "which pages rest on its claims" are different questions, and collapsing them would
silently shrink the graph. Verified: SC5 stayed at 707 edges / 28 pages across every annotation.

Then the write-up tripped its own gate — naming an entry in prose *is* a citation, so the SC12-FIND
task flagged `intake-896`, `intake-110`, `intake-991`, `intake-922`, `intake-346` from its own text.
That is the convention working, and it is now documented in `CLAUDE.md` so the next audit note does
not rediscover it.

### The real findings

| entry | document | what was wrong |
|---|---|---|
| intake-110 | `reasoning-compression.md`, `wiki/cost-aware-routing.md` | asserted "+9–16 points" accuracy uplift the authors **revised away** |
| intake-346 | `wiki/memory-augmented.md` | called Mem0 a "$24M **cloud** memory platform" |

OPSDC's real numbers, from the authors' current Table 2: Qwen3-8B MATH-500 95.7 → 95.7 (**+0.0**,
56.9% reduction), Qwen3-14B 93.0 → 96.3 (**+3.3**, 56.3%). Per their own Appendix D the "+9–16" was
the *base* model mis-scored by a boxed-only grader. Compression stands; accuracy is parity.

The Mem0 one mattered beyond wording. The 2026-08-07 dive overturned exactly the "cloud-only"
proposition — the Apache-2.0 repo self-hosts via Ollama, LiteLLM, configurable OpenAI-compatible
endpoints and local vector stores. Left uncorrected, **"cloud-only" would have disqualified Mem0
under the open-source/self-hosted-only sourcing policy** — a wrong sourcing decision, not a stale
sentence.

### A grading defect underneath intake-110

intake-110 was the **only conflicted claim in 4,233 beliefs**, and the reason was the adapter, not a
live dispute. A per-claim `claim_corrections` verdict of `overturned` flipped the **direction** but
inherited the **entry-level Q**. intake-110 is the only entry in the corpus carrying a per-claim
overturn with no entry-level `dive-overturned`, so its refutation was recorded at `Hinted` — tied
with the stage-1 support it refutes.

`apply_claim_verdict()` now raises an overturn to at least `Verified` (never moving T, never
downgrading a stronger entry-level verdict) and is called by **both** the emitter and the run report,
which had already drifted apart once (112 opposition reported vs 106 emitted). One frame re-ingested;
`clm_intake_110_04` is now `pro=Hinted/Located con=Verified/Located`.

### Self-inflicted, caught and fixed

That ingest ran `--as-of 2026-08-11` on 2026-08-10 — the same class of defect the earlier session
corrected 40 stamps for, in a program whose subject is provenance. Append-only means it could not be
removed: the assertion was re-appended at the true date and the future-dated frame retracted. It also
exposed **SC17** — `fold` does not exclude frames dated after `as_of`, so a mis-stamped frame applies
immediately. That is an inherited accident, not a choice.
