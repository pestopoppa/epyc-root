## Registry validator was failing closed for everyone (`a94e0e01`)

`ternary_bonsai_27b_q2_g64.performance` had **two `evidence:` keys in the same mapping**. PyYAML keeps
the last, so an earlier list was discarded on every parse and **two artifact paths were being silently
lost**. This failed the validator on the whole file, so no registry change anywhere could be validated.

reports 0 problems.**

Follow-ups for whoever owns registry hygiene: the validator is not in any gate (this survived since
`5e4ec862`), and path style is unenforced (the same artifact appeared once absolute, once relative,
which is what made the duplicate non-obvious).
