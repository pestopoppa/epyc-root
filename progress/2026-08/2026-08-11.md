### R1 — the nudge guard hardened as the condition worsened (P0, `b1222b6e`)

everything else I owned. **This, not a missing coordinator tick, is why the fleet stalled for ten

The daemon calls a heartbeat older than 3600s STUCK and nudges; the adapter refused every nudge past
900s. Between the two thresholds nobody has decided you are stuck; past 3600s somebody has and can
no longer reach you — **the guard tightened exactly as the condition got worse**. Every main crossed
900s at ~10:14-10:22Z and the whole fleet, coordinator included, went unreachable: 1,903
`stuck-nudge-refused` rows, recovered only by a human passing `--heartbeat-max-age 86400` by hand.

Both documented escape hatches were unavailable, which is why nothing self-healed: C35 lifts the
`working` blocker and never staleness, and C36 is codex-rollout-only — 0% on an all-Claude fleet.

**What I ruled out.** Raising the default max-age. It closes the deadlock and opens a worse hazard —
typing into a pane that is genuinely mid-generation. Staleness is a *timer*, and a timer cannot
distinguish wedged from quietly waiting. The pane can, so the pane decides: `pane_dead` false plus
quiescence past the spinner interval, which is exactly the evidence C35 already trusts for a
`working` self-report. Fails closed on every unknown.

**A test was asserting the bug.** `test_c35_the_override_touches_only_the_working_blocker` justified
never overriding staleness on the grounds that it "is already tunable with `--heartbeat-max-age`" —
the very knob a human had to set to 86400 to rescue the fleet. Rewritten, intent preserved.

**The fail-open that hid it, and the reason it ran 1,903 times in silence:** `last_nudge_ts` /
`last_nudge_sig` are written only on `rc == 0`, and the escalation is gated on `last_nudge_sig`. An
