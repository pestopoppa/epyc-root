**What the batch turned out to be about.** Operator steering reframed it from "what do these sources

campaigns, partitioned CPU screening, frontier-API planners, no per-run approval) into robust

T1→T3 and screen→verify are one object, and it is one field on the evaluation event that is free to
record now and impossible to reconstruct later.

**Three of my own assertions were corrected by the operator and are now recorded in the handoff**:
AutoKernel experiments need no per-run approval (the human boundary is freeze/cutover/promotion);
CPU and GPU campaigns can run concurrently (they are separate resource claims); and the loop's
objective is experiment churn, not aggregate throughput — so deep partitioning costs nothing that
matters here, only rank inversion does.

- The C2 "reference" in `test-backend-ops` is a **sibling implementation**, not an independent one —
  `ggml-cpu.c:3041` shows `use_ref` disables fusion/tiling on the same type traits and dequantisers.
