### CH-9 — resolved, and my first diagnosis was wrong

I published that `mul_mat_id` blindness explained DF2-6's empty route log. It did not: after
instrumenting `mul_mat_id` AND adding a log-every-type level, the log was **still empty**. The real
cause is that common's log callback filters backend `GGML_LOG_INFO` at default verbosity —
`ggml_cuda_init` is visible only because it logs before that callback is installed. `--verbose`
gives 4705 route lines from a 24-token generation. The fix was a runtime flag, not code.

The evidence DF2-6 mandated is now captured on all four arms, and it shows `a6b4b5263`'s rule
firing exactly as written: **every speculative verify batch takes MMQ (ne11≥2) while greedy decode
takes MMVQ (ne11=1)**. That patch is "numerically-valid (not bit-exact)" by its own commit message,
so it is now a *directly evidenced* candidate cause of DF2-6's non-parity.

**A gate caught my over-reach.** The first CH-9 commit also edited `mmvq.cu`; validation refused it
with `production/instrument reviewed target differs`. `_TARGET_SOURCE_SHA256` pins files that must
be byte-identical between production and instrument because those are the planner's **mutation
targets** — an instrument that pre-modifies one corrupts attribution at the root. Reverted; only
`ggml-cuda.cu` (not a mutation target) remains.

## Fourth pass — the planner was flying blind (operator asked me to dig further)

I had noted v33's repeated `authoring_refused` and moved on. Asked to investigate, the answer was
worth the dig — and my stated suspicion was wrong.

**Not the do-not-repeat ledger.** That works: `portfolio_authoring_failures` reached 3, the
hypothesis was skipped with `scientific_terminal: false`, and provider transients are correctly
excluded from the strike count.

**The real defect: `prior_authoring_refusals` filtered on `status == "planner_refused"`,** a status
written almost never. The status a rejected diff actually gets is `authoring_refused`. Across
v28–v34: **`authoring_refused` 22, `planner_refused` 1** — so for 22 of 23 authoring failures the
planner was told nothing about why its diff was rejected, and re-derived rejected work blind. The
field's name says `authoring_refusals`; its predicate excluded the status of that name.

v33's cost: two different diffs, byte-identical refusal, three strikes, hypothesis retired for the
campaign **without ever being tested**. v34 reproduced it on turn 1.

Fixed with `AUTHORING_REFUSAL_STATUSES` (research `9516ac05`), regression-tested on v33's real rows
and mutation-tested (reverting to the old predicate fails exactly the two tests that should catch
it). Broader suite unchanged, 86 passed. **v34 stopped and relaunched as v35** on the fix rather
than letting it burn 100 turns blind.
