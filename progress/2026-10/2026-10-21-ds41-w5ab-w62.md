## W5a: NMSE spot checks

- **Weight fidelity, done without compute.** The HF 1017 experts are OCP MXFP4: `I8` packed E2M1 with an
  `F8_E8M0` scale per 32 elements. In antirez's GGUF the codes and scales are bit-identical to the source on
  4/4 checked expert matrices, so NMSE = 0. Q8_0 non-experts vs their FP8 E4M3 source measure NMSE about
  2.9e-5 to 3.0e-5. F16 vs F32 measures about 4e-8. These come from about 50 MB of HTTP range reads and
  single-thread numpy.
- **Kernel fidelity.** The AK served-shape MXFP4 NMSE = 0.0 is vacuous for the served path: `test-backend-ops`
  never allocates a CPU_REPACK buffer, so it never runs `ggml_gemv_mxfp4_8x8_q8_0`. I wrote a real-weight
  CPU_REPACK MUL_MAT_ID harness (`data/ds41-1017-w5-20261021/w5a/mxfp4_repack_nmse.cpp`). It runs in the slot
  because q0-q3 were held exclusive by kvu12-redo, so no build claim was available.
