   failure was never reported. Fix: watcher v2 keeps its stamp across re-arms and advances it only to the time of
   the find that fired.

   place 0, so the check read "0" against "0-95". The server itself was healthy. Diagnosis sent to 3c4ca599, which
   fixed it (the union of all thread masks must lie within the grant's CPUs) and bumped `PIN_SHA` a6a1d30c →

   made the driver permanently skip arm t48_r1.
