## Findings from source (anchor `06b169e27` = champion `dd3d9721c` + a test commit)

- **MXFP4 does not run on iqk.** Under `GGML_IQK=1` it is in neither the repack-diversion list (`repack.cpp:4547-4570`)
  nor `iqk_typeA_supported` (`iqk_dispatch.cpp:128`). The 1017 experts therefore run CPU_REPACK `mxfp4_8x8_q8_0`, and
  none of the champion's MMID keeps (all in `ggml_iqk_try_mul_mat_id`) reach them.
- **Repack `forward_mul_mat_id` calls `gemv` once per routed row** (`repack.cpp:4530`) and never `gemm`, unlike the
  dense path. This is the likely cause of the pp512 gap (87.94 vs 125.39 t/s, research `da71fa603`).
  - It quantizes src1 on only 1–6 threads at decode (`:4461`).
  - Thread 0 alone builds the row map (`:4474`).
- **The MXFP4 GEMV template is ymm-only, with a single accumulator and 8 scalar E8M0 lookups per block.** It is shared
  with Q4_0 and IQ4_NL. Q38FN serves IQ4_NL through it, so every change must be type-gated.
- **Champion vs port `3376147f8`.** The ggml diff touches only `ggml-cpu.c`, `iqk_dispatch.cpp` and `sgemm.cpp`: four
  Q38FN keeps. The repack kernels are byte-identical, so the W5 timing evidence applies to the anchor.
