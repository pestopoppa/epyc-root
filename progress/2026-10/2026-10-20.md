## 2026-10-20 UTC — Q38FN CPU serving: thread count x context depth sweep + AK long-context harness fix

- Operator question: why half the cores idle on the :8074 CPU server. But the 48-thread optimum was only ever measured at ~0-530 tokens of context and pre-BIOS.
- Stopped :8074 (operator-authorized), saved its state + full stdout. Measured (binary cpu-20261004-b0ba1d427, UD-IQ4_XS + shared MTP, production env, slot-restore at depth, wikitext2): evidence research `data/q38fn-threads-depth-20261020/` (commit 23119ea4e; SUMMARY.md), llama-bench rows at `data/cpu-model-characterization/q38fn-ud-iq4xs-b0ba1d427-20261020/llama_bench_stdout.json`.
  - llama-bench d0 (no MTP): pp512 t48 212 / t96 231 (+9%); tg128 t48 31.2 / t96 24.8 (-21%).
  - In this binary `-tb` governs every ubatch with n_tokens>1 (llama-context.cpp:1482), i.e. prefill AND MTP verify; `-t` only single-token steps.
  - Served prefill of a 4k tail: -tb96 +21-29% at shallow depth (198-211 vs 164 t/s); from 8k depth up all arms within the ~7% session drift band (162-170 @8k ... 71-75 @128k).
  - Served decode with MTP: no thread effect resolvable at >=32k depth (identical greedy outputs across arms; 29-30 @32k, 25 @64k, 16-17 @128k); session drift up to ~10%.
  - Depth is the cost: decode -55%, prefill -60% from shallow to 128k, insensitive to 48 vs 96 threads -> the lever is the attention kernel / QSA realization (AK inbox #64/#67), not thread count.
  - At 128k the raw-completion probe puts ~98% on EOS as the first token (vs `<think>` top at 64k); forced output is coherent and identical across arms - model behaviour on a raw prompt, not corruption.
  - Greedy+MTP outputs differ between launches at shallow depth (known spec-decode non-exactness), identical at >=32k.
  - Serving recommendation: `-t 48 -tb 96`. Hand to AK as a runtime arm for adoption.
