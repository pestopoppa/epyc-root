## Q38FN long-context finding (INF-81, AKX-ALL-23/24)
- **Structural, code-reading only, not benched.** Q38FN (GGUF arch `qwen4exp`: 48 layers, full attention every
  4th layer = 12 attn layers, 24 Q heads, 2 KV heads, head_dim 256) runs QSA sparse attention with indexer
  `top_k=2048` (indexer 4 heads, key_length 128, compressed blocks). In `src/models/qwen4exp.cpp`
  (`build_qsa_top_k` / `build_attn_qsa`, ~L675-880) the top-k is realized by `ggml_set_rows` unmasking cells in a
  FULL `n_kv` KQ mask, then dense FA over all `n_kv` cells — so decode cost may still be **O(n_kv)** despite
  `top_k=2048`. Q38FN decode-vs-context is **UNMEASURED**. Structural lever if it is not flat: gather the top-k
  K/V rows into an O(2048) buffer before FA. Filed as AKX-ALL-23 in
  `handoffs/active/autokernel-all-devices-all-dimensions.md`.
- **Correction:** an earlier AK inbox note wrongly attributed workspace-ec's Qwen3.6-35B-A3B YaRN CPU-leg decode
  curve (20.4 → 2.9 tok/s, 34K → 323K) to Q38FN. Corrected in `store-b0ba1d427/inbox/64-*.md`. Qwen3.6-35B-A3B
  (dense attention) and Q38FN (QSA) are architecturally different models despite being `qwen4exp`-family
  siblings; the curve does not transfer.
- Re-verified against INF-59 (`yarn-context-extension-research.md`, workspace-ec's own 2026-10-05 CPU-leg entry):
  native vs YaRN Qwen3.6 decode matched within noise at every shared depth (34K ~21, 133K ~6.7, 253K ~4.0 tok/s;
