**C96 (research `e29dca63`) — root cause of the graph-sync gate fault.**
- `_DS41_SYNC_OPS` passed `GLU` and `UNARY` to `test-backend-ops -o`, which matches `op_desc()`, i.e. the
  sub-op names (SILU, SWIGLU, ...). The suite was therefore always 0/0, deterministically since 09-28 (rows
  108/109/115 predate C84).
- Fix: `backend_ops_selector` expands UNARY to its 20 sub-ops and GLU to 6. EXP/EXPM1 are excluded because
  they fail on the anchor itself (inputs up to ±150 overflow).
- The route now covers 29 ops, adding SUB/DIV/SQR/SQRT/LOG/SIN/COS/FILL/DUP and TOPK_MOE (TOPK_MOE exercises
  the multi-node GET_ROWS→SUM_ROWS→CLAMP→DIV chain). The empty suite still fails closed. Probes on
  anchor-gen-011 all passed.
- Caveat: the op-level oracle cannot see deterministic walk defects shared by both arms. Follow-up: an e2e
  `--autokernel-harden` output-hash comparison (DS41-C99).
