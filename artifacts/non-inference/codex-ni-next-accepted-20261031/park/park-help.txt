usage: python -m src.runtime.gpu_window park [-h] [--roles ROLES]
                                             [--ports PORTS]
                                             [--holder {autokernel,released}]
                                             --expected-end EXPECTED_END

park only writes the window file; it does NOT stop servers or free the GPU. To
stop servers, use gpu_window_executor open with a schedule entry and compute
grant (60 min default cap).

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --roles ROLES         comma-separated roles
  --ports PORTS         comma-separated ports
  --holder {autokernel,released}
  --expected-end EXPECTED_END
