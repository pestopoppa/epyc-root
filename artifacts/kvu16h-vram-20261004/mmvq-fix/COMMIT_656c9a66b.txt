ggml-cuda: give the HIP-graph MMVQ q8_1 cache a private per-context arena

The graph-cached MMVQ q8_1 buffers (Q8_0/Q4_K/Q6_K, ne11==1, stream 0) were
held as ggml_cuda_pool_alloc's from the shared ctx.pool() from capture until
the next capture on the context. With GGML_HIP_NO_VMM the legacy pool is
best-fit, whole-buffer, 256-slot and never shrinks, so a batch-1 capture's
~250 small holds consumed every free pool buffer, including the large cuBLAS
F16 dequant buffers; each following >128-token prefill cudaMalloc'd a fresh
set, and once >=256 buffers were held the overflow cudaFree + re-drain made
VRAM grow linearly per decode/prefill alternation (KVU-16h, :8083 51.69 ->
59.77 GiB). The legacy pool could also cudaFree a released buffer (slot
overflow, OOM-retry clear_pool) that a captured graph still references.

The cache now carves exact-size, 256-byte aligned slices out of a private
device arena per context (4 MiB chunks), rewound at each new capture and
freed only when the context is destroyed. It never touches ctx.pool(); its
footprint is bounded by the largest single capture's q8_1 demand; every
address a live graph instance was captured with stays allocated. Kernels,
launch order and q8_1 contents are unchanged (allocation only).
