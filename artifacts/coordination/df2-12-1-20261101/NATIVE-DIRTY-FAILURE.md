## Source audit and next experiment

The frozen test decodes an original prompt into `ctx_src`, removes its last three positions, serializes its sequence checkpoint, and compares full and partial restores to a fresh `ctx_dst`. It then decodes unrelated tokens into `ctx_dirty`, rolls that back, loads the original checkpoint over the dirty state, and compares replay logits. The failure is at the first dirty replay; earlier comparisons returned success. `common_prompt_checkpoint::load_tgt` calls `llama_state_seq_set_data_ext`; `llama_context::state_seq_read_data` calls the hybrid attention and recurrent `state_read` functions. This narrows the investigation to restore over existing sequence state or the test's assumed equivalence, but does not yet identify a faulty line. In particular, the exact mismatch is not evidence of a production 35B/27B parity failure or a regression relative to a separately measured baseline.

Champion owner should first audit existing current experimental branch fixes against this frozen-source path and reproduce this small-model test on the owner candidate with its native CPU/GPU DSOs. A minimal repair, if needed, belongs in the **existing experimental champion**, followed by dirty-context PASS and paired output controls on the final same-source image. The frozen source tree and kernel store remain untouched. Until this gate passes, calibration and 304-request science remain unissued.
