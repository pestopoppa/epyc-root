# CH-17 candidate promotion requirement — draft, not a gate

The current kernel-promotion scope derives the in-service lineup and its load-only step checks each already selected model/drafter pair. That does not by itself compare all eligible drafter choices or document why each role's selection changed or remained unchanged. This draft is not an implemented rule, authorization to run inference, or a modification to `promotion_gates.yaml`.

At each promotion, before ratification, derive the in-scope served roles from the promotion scope. Snapshot and hash the topology and model registry used to derive them. For each role, record the target model, backend, current drafter selection, supported speculative types from the exact candidate's `llama-server --help`, and eligible drafters from the role's registry. Bind the capability observation to the candidate's commit and binary SHA, and represent absent/malformed registry data or an unobservable capability as unknown rather than an empty set. Do not infer that a model or its drafter can load from a help string alone.

For each eligible choice, attach the applicable candidate qualification evidence and its fixed workload, anchor, decision rule, custody, quality and measured limits. Identify a winner only where the existing predeclared rules and evidence support it; if evidence is missing, say so and leave the affected selection decision unresolved. The outcome for each role must be either a reviewed `stack_topology.yaml` `drafter_selection` change or an explicit `unchanged, because ...` citing the relevant evidence. The ratification package must carry these outcomes, the inputs/identities and evidence pointers, and refuse a missing in-scope role or unresolved selection before freeze. Do not turn this into a new grading rule; the project measurement constitution and claim ladder remain authoritative.

Validation design, before implementation: pure fixture cases for complete scope with a changed selection, an unchanged selection with a reason, and a no-drafter role where explicitly supported; negative controls for omitted role, duplicate/conflicting role, missing reason, absent candidate-binary identity, stale registry/topology snapshot, missing evidence, unsupported candidate spec type, and candidate/anchor mismatch. Include a version of the v10 trigger: DFlash2 is viable on a role currently selected for MTP, so load-only on MTP must not satisfy the comparison/decision record. Prove failure before packaging and no production or topology write in those negative controls. The policy for how evidence establishes eligibility and the final signed receipt field names must follow the actual promotion and measurement contracts rather than a new ad hoc schema.
