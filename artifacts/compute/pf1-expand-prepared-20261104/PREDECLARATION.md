Purpose: measure CPU versus MI210 prefill throughput for Qwen3.8-Flash-Next UD-IQ4_XS at shorter L. This is a diagnostic observation on the authorized C6 candidate build, not production or a serving decision.

Each cell is one llama-bench process/model load; all cells use `-mmp 0 --load-threads 96`; GPU-skew policy remains the operator's 2026-11-03 amendment (GPU requires valid in-window sample and >=90% RSS coverage, max node share is a covariate; A0 remains strict <=40%).

Every other cell uses `-r 1` per invocation and has five independent launch-level observations. The controller uses the already reviewed 10,000-resample median percentile bootstrap, seed 34, 2.5%/97.5% bounds. Provisional L* is the smallest tested L at which the best A1/A2 arm/ub median is >=1.10× A0 and its 95% CI does not overlap A0's. No new threshold or grading rule.

The holdout is a separate later session, starts >=4 hours after a complete initial receipt, uses the same frozen manifest and reverses arm/ub order at the two lengths bracketing L*. If no L* appears in the forward shorter-L frame, the existing P3-3 conditional holdout lengths remain 8192 and 32768. An L* result is not final without holdout agreement. The old P3-3 packet explicitly leaves all protocol IDs empty unless every protocol condition is satisfied; otherwise output remains diagnostic, with no scheduler/adoption/NPD/closure decision. P-GPU-1 fresh-server conditions are not established by llama-bench.

The existing VB-PREFILL-XOVER write-side source and belief family are the carrier. Each native cell writes a self-hashed ClaimTuple-shaped OBSERVATION record; no grade is created by this packet.
