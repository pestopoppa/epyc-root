Effects are screening thresholds from the approved plan, not equivalence or statistical guarantees. At n=4 each individual quality flip is 25 percentage points, so this probe can identify clear counterexamples and choose whether to continue; it cannot resolve a one-in-32 guard. No variance estimate is assumed: timing error is judged against observed paired spread and reported as descriptive, not as a confidence interval or a formal sequential test. High spread at cap is inconclusive. Stop on answer or futility, preserve partial records; timeout/interruption is not a decision. All limits are ceilings, not target workloads. Runtime measured from first authorized stage unit, not from scheduler reservation. Persist native item records and decisions incrementally; stop between units and require a fresh explicit controller command to continue; a predeclared mechanical decision suffices, with no language-model review required per item. No automatic successor at a look or stop.
