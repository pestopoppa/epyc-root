# ID-4 real episode snapshot

This snapshot prepares real optimization episodes, not synthetic alpha fixtures. The read-only SQLite backup contains all 1,428 original rows. Both canonical journal shards are copied verbatim, and `folded-journal.json` is the native ExperimentJournal supersession view (1,372 trials). Nonfinite floats in that JSON are explicitly tagged `original_nonfinite_float`; raw shards retain original bytes. No live StrategyStore was instantiated, and no inference ran.

The conservative recorded-provenance filter retains 450 raw episodes, with 346 source and 104 held-out episodes. It rejects nonraw abstractions, unknown/excluded trial evidence, ambiguous family and visible #trial references absent from structured provenance. Rejected rows remain auditable. Original IDs/species/text/metadata are unchanged. Species is never used as task family. Recorded `action_type` defines *optimization* task families: seed_batch 325/101, numeric_trial 6/2, structural_experiment 8/1, deep_eval 6/0, consult_gate_probe 1/0 (source/heldout). Seed batches are actual historical evaluation runs, but this does not make their aggregate summaries useful knowledge.

Connected components of structured evidence trial IDs are split chronologically per family (last 20% held out; families with fewer than five components receive no holdout). Both episode IDs and underlying recorded evidence trial IDs are disjoint. `source-episodes.json` and `heldout-episodes.json` wrap the untouched native row in provenance/family fields; a runner must pass the inner `episode` to the paired primitive. `family-split.json` names all memberships. Distillation invocations must be isolated per family while preserving native species. A family with fewer than three same-species compatible source records cannot pass the native minimum cluster requirement; the family-level size flag alone is not a manipulation proof.

This is a preparation artifact, **not a ready quality benchmark**. Aggregate journal scores are historical outcomes of different optimization actions; they are not correctness labels for answers to new questions. No benchmark question text, stable question identity and answer oracle were exported here. Nor does absence of visible #references prove a natural-language summary cites no other trial: semantic provenance review is still required. Parent/source relationships and repeated seed-batch questions may also connect the underlying benchmark evidence even when trial IDs differ. The zero recorded-trial overlap therefore establishes recorded provenance disjointness only, not independence of underlying benchmark questions. These limitations must not be converted into a downstream nonregression claim.

Next executable preparation is to join held-out trials to immutable evaluation question/result artifacts, freeze question identities/prompts/oracles and question-level train/holdout disjointness, and create pre-outcome queries from hypothesis/reasoning without exposing insights, outcomes, config changes made after results, or scorer labels. If those artifacts cannot be recovered, collect a fresh held-out benchmark from canonical suites rather than fabricate labels. consult_gate_probe needs additional independent episodes; deep_eval lacks a distinct held-out evidence component. Do not silently omit these families when reporting overall quality.

A CPU retrieval-only pilot may characterize native retrieval and distillation counters, with no adoption verdict. Explicit manipulation failures are zero patterns/conventions, unchanged retrieved context, missing input provenance, evidence leakage, or failed invalid-evidence quarantine. Pin code hashes, actual embedder bytes/tokenizer/dimension and CPU placement. The provided snapshot is an input export, not a measurement producer or measured claim, and no new Vidya grading ladder is defined.

## Candidate decision rule (proposal to freeze with the completed benchmark)

A defensible conservative default is Pareto improvement: candidate quality must be strictly higher and total pipeline token cost no higher, with no family harmed. Operationalize this *before execution* with paired per-question correctness (higher is better), total pipeline tokens including distillation amortized across a declared reuse horizon (lower is better), identical context budgets and model/decoding/scoring pins, and family-stratified paired confidence intervals. Require the 95% lower confidence bound on overall quality delta to exceed 0, every family lower bound to be at least 0, and the 95% upper bound on cost delta to be at most 0. Predeclare resampling method, multiplicity correction and minimum sample size/power against a stated practical effect before the run. Inconclusive intervals mean no candidate selection, not equivalence. CPU wall time and peak memory remain separately reported, not hidden in token cost.

This strict zero-tradeoff rule needs no invented acceptable quality loss or monetary exchange rate, but the existing 104 holdouts dominated by seed_batch do not support its interpretation as a downstream quality test. It is a candidate proposal; no benchmark-specific adoption rule can honestly be ratified from these aggregate records alone. Production caller wiring and adoption remain outside this preparation.
